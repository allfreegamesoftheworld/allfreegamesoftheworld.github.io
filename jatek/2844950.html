Röviden a játékról

Jumping Steve is a 2d arcade style indy casual roguelike game. Stay on the crows. Collect the thingys to increase your score so you can unlock stuff. MOD machine randomness. With over 2 Achievements. Power ups. 2 player. Lots of items. Visit Camp Aign. Not compatible with the gameboy camera.
