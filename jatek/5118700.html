Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

The mature content in this game relates to the themes of its stories, which deal with death, loss, and separation. The game is told in text and still images, and contains no graphic or violent imagery. Players who may be sensitive to the following topics should be aware of them before playing: suicide, the death of a young person, grief, and the loss of a close relationship.
