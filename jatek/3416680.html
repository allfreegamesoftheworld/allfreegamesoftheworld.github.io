Röviden a játékról

OWL is top down, rogue like bullet-hell that you can play with up to 1 friend. Everything is in beat, including your and the enemies attacks. With each new Instrument, new attack patterns unfold for you which will take you to your final obstacle; The Meowestro, the source of dissonance.
