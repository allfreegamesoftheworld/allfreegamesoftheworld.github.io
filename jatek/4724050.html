Röviden a játékról

Idlemoor is a dark fantasy idle MMORPG set in a persistent online realm. Trade on the marketplace, fight in battlegrounds, defend the town in community events, and chat with fellow Wanderers! Infinite build possibilities, where each item is completely unique. Will your build be the best?
