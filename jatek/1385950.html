Röviden a játékról

Guilt Free is a visual novel crossed with a point and click made to offer perspective on mental health and eating disorders. Navigate a couple's rocky relationship and encounter &quot;flaws&quot; or &quot;quirks&quot; that may actually be symptomatic behaviors.

Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Please be advised, that the game features content which may be unsuitable for some players. The main storyline revolves around mental health issues and eating disorders. Depending on the story flow, the game may also include mentions of alcohol use and non-consensual sex. All of these are textual mentions and include no visual representation.
