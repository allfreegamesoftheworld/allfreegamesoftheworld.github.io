One Map is an online shooter where the entire world fights in the same never-ending battle. No lobbies, no waiting, just instant action. The dynamic map adjusts to the player count, ensuring non-stop combat. Survive, compete, and help break the world record for the most players online in a game.

Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

One Map is a third person shooter featuring online multiplayer battles. Players engage in PVP combat using firearms to kill each other in order to stay alive longer, but the game does not include blood, gore, or dismemberment. There are no depictions of sexual content, drug use, or self-harm.
