This game is a fantasy MMORPG and contains the following mature content: - Fantasy Violence: Players battle monsters and bosses using skills and weapons, with attack effects, hit reactions, and on-screen damage numbers. The violence is stylized and non-realistic — there is no gore, blood, or dismemberment. - Suggestive Themes: Some character illustrations depict stylized fantasy characters wearing revealing or suggestive outfits. The game contains no nudity, no explicit sexual content, and no depiction of sexual acts. - In-Game Purchases and Randomized Items: The game offers optional in-game purchases and includes a randomized item acquisition system (probability-based "Synchro Chip" draws). All drop rates are disclosed in-game. This game does NOT contain depictions of sexual assault or non-consensual sexual content, drug or alcohol abuse, or self-harm. No content of that nature is present, and therefore no such warnings are required.
