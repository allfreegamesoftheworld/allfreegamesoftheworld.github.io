Röviden a játékról

Deal with the Deep is a single-player roguelike deckbuilder featuring positional card combat and a tabletop route system. Choose a character, descend into the abyss, use movement, range and facing to outmaneuver enemies, and shape each run through cards, relics, events and route choices.
