Röviden a játékról

Trash Time is a virtual reality game that can teach you how to recycle in a fun way. Burnt trash accumulates CO2 gas, that is one of the leading causes of global warming. Try to save the Earth by playing as a alien robot with flexible hands, recycle trash using powerups and power tools.
