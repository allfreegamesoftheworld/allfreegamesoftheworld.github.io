Röviden a játékról

Andromeda 2 Zombies is an First Person Shooter video game featuring zombies. The game take place in the far future where spatial exploration of galaxy is possible. A virus has spread and now zombies are rampant. The hero is in the military and try to bring order in the Andromeda galaxy.
