Röviden a játékról

Taka Taka is a Japanese-folklore-inspired rhythm game. Play as Tengu and Oni, an unlikely pair attempting to survive a world of Yokai. Match the characters with their colorized notes and face increasingly more conniving enemies in order to progress through the world of Yokai.
