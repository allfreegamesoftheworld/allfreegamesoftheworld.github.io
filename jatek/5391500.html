Röviden a játékról

Ardhan is a roguelike deckbuilder where every fight happens on a real board: minions trade, taunts hold the line, weapons break, and the deck you walk out with is never the deck you walked in with. 12 factions, 421 cards, 6 acts, Ascension 1–15 and a Daily Game shared by everyone.
