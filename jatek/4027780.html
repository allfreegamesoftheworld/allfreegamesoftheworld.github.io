Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

The game is a simulation of a Slot Machine, in which the player gambles their score to try to be at the top of the leaderboard worldwide. Player is also able to use the score to buy skins (only in-game, no steam inventory connectivity). The game does not offer any real-money winnings or top-up of any kind, it's just a fake game score.
