Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Contains text-based tactical combat, fictional violence, injury and incapacitation descriptions, death and reconstruction themes, threatening creatures, coercive institutions, political conflict, cult themes, and identity, possession, or memory uncertainty. Violence may include occasional descriptions of bodily harm, but it is presented through text and a stylized interface rather than graphic visuals. The game contains no sexual content, nudity, gambling, or in-game purchases.
