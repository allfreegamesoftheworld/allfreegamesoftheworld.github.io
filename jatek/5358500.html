Röviden a játékról

Start, rev and shift 19 engines, from a Harley V-twin to a Formula 1 H16, each turning in 3D in front of you. Every sound is computed live from the simulated physics of the engine. Clutch, gas and an H-pattern on keyboard, mouse or gamepad.
