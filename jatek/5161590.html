Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Deadly Force contains frequent arcade-style gun violence. Players shoot armed human enemies using firearms during combat encounters. The game includes blood effects, depictions of people being shot and killed, terrorist and criminal themes, illegal drugs, hostage situations, hijacking scenarios, and armed attacks in public and government locations. The violence is presented in a stylized arcade-shooter format and is not intended to realistically simulate injury or trauma. Authorized Use Of DEADLY FORCE!
