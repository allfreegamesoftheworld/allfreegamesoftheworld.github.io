Röviden a játékról

Dive into AquaRevive, a VR game in a glass dome battling algal bloom pollution. Armed with a dynamic chemical ball, you must destroy illuminated targets to save the ecosystem. Face waves of challenges, testing your speed and accuracy in a race against time.
