Röviden a játékról

A turn-based local LLM-powered narrative battle sim where you command the action. Over 200 animals from across the kingdom Animalia are mutated by eight unique types. Make your monsters fight and then battle your friends over Bluetooth. No two battles play the same way. No internet required.
