Röviden a játékról

FreeState: San Francisco is a free-to-play open-world driving game where you can explore a city inspired by San Francisco behind the wheel of a wide variety of vehicles. Cruise through downtown streets, highways, hills and scenic coastal roads while discovering everything the city has to offer.
