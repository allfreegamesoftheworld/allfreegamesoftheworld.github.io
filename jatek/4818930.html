Röviden a játékról

Play as a copy of Theseus. Run around in a maze, having to defeat the very Minotaur in Tartarus after it perished by the hands of the original Theseus. Over and over again. The soul of the Minotaur can't rest. All it can do is chase the player and try to outsmart you.
