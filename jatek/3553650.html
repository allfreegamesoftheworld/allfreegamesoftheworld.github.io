Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Dream Slayers features stylized fantasy combat and dark thematic elements set within a dreamlike world. Players will encounter depictions of combat, death, and corruption presented in a surreal, non-realistic style. The game does not include any sexual content, nudity, depictions of sexual assault, drug or alcohol abuse, or self-harm. While the tone can be dark and intense at times, all mature content is designed to support the game’s atmosphere and story rather than shock or disturb.
