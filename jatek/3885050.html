Röviden a játékról

FOR GOD'S SAKE, HELP HIM! is a tiny, surreal first-person art &quot;game&quot; about what it's like. your goal is singular and simple: try to help him, however you can... if you can. that's a tall order considering you're both in a blank void and he's being crushed over and over.
