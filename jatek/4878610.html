Röviden a játékról

The sunken sea-beast Snugfang is waking and its tide is rising. 1–4 players — solo brings AI Chums along to scramble for the ONE buoy above the flood; it holds two. Betray for the last seat and go under laughing. A physics party-brawl where who drowns last wins. Couch or online.
