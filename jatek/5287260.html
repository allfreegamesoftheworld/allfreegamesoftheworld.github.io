Röviden a játékról

Pactmage: Chess Roster is a turn-based tactics game where every character has skills and an element. Win in Adventure Mode and form a Pact to recruit them — all 60+ units are free. Play the story mode, fight a friend locally, or battle online in PvP. No gacha, no paid units.
