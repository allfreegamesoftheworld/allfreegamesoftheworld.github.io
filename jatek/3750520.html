Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

This game includes detective mystery elements involving murder cases. It features deceased characters, but no scenes of violence or depictions of the act of murder are shown. There is no gore or graphic content. Victims are shown as still bodies for investigation purposes, and murder weapons appear as bloodless evidence. The focus is on deduction and mystery-solving rather than violent imagery.
