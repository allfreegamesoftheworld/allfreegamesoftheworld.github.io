"In Helbreath, players will encounter frequent instances of violence, including combat between characters using various weapons and magic spells. Additionally, the game features mature themes such as warfare, conquest, and political intrigue within a fantasy setting. Players may also encounter mild instances of suggestive themes and language during interactions with NPCs and other players. It's important to note that while the game does not contain explicit nudity or graphic sexual content, the overall tone and atmosphere may not be suitable for all audiences."
