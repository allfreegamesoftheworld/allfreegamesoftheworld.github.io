Röviden a játékról

Welcome to Dash Dash Delivery, a simulation where you play as a delivery person in a nearby mall. Have fun delivering items, exploring, purchasing items, customizing your character, enjoying delicious mall food items, as well as the upbeat atmosphere. Can you find all the easter eggs?
