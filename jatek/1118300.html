A fantasy yuri visual novel in which a noblewoman uncovers her father's evil plot and, along with her lowborn friends, takes to the seas to flee from high society. Her plan to right her family's wrongs and return home are disrupted by the untimely arrival of an enigmatic and flirtatious pirate.
