Röviden a játékról

Mayflower Reflections is an exciting VR film that explores the story of the Mayflower Voyage of 1620. Hear the captivating story of the Plymouth Colony as you are immersed in their journey. Become inspired as you learn how the actions of these early travellers laid the groundwork for modern America.
