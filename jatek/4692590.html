Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Veles: Rise of the Damned is a dark fantasy action-survival game featuring combat against supernatural enemies including undead, demons, wolves, spiders, and vampires. Violence: The game contains stylized fantasy violence. Players use abilities to defeat waves of enemies. Combat involves creatures being destroyed, with no realistic gore or graphic depictions of injury. Violence is gameplay-focused and arcade in nature, consistent with the genre (similar to Vampire Survivors). Themes: The game explores dark fantasy themes including death, the undead, damnation, and supernatural evil. Enemy types include skeletons, reapers, wraiths, and a Vampire Lord. Ability names and progression systems reference darkness and damnation as thematic flavor. There is no sexual content, nudity, drug use, self-harm, or depictions of real-world violence in this game.
