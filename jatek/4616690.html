Röviden a játékról

Where is your cat flying right now? Outta Space is an idle game where a tiny cat explores the universe from your desktop corner. Every keystroke, every click fuels the spaceship. From the Moon, past the solar system, to distant stars. Come back and see how far your lovely cat has gone.
