Minimális gépigény

Minimum:
  • Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system
  • OS: Windows 10 64-bit
  • Processor: Intel Core i3-6100 or AMD Ryzen 3 1200 (2 cores, 3 GHz)
  • Memory: 4096 MB RAM
  • Graphics: NVIDIA GeForce GTX 950 / AMD Radeon RX 460 (2 GB), or Intel Iris Xe integrated graphics
  • DirectX: Version 11
  • Network: Broadband Internet connection
  • Storage: 500 MB available space
  • Sound Card: DirectX-compatible
