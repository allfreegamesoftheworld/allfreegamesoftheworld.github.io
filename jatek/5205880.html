Röviden a játékról

Undealt is an idle RPG that lives in the corner of your screen. Four heroes fight on their own while you work or play — you come back to equip the loot, forge better gear, learn skills and collect cards that rewrite the rules of combat. Free to play.

Minimális gépigény

Minimum:
  • Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system
  • OS: Windows 10 64-bit
  • Processor: Dual-core 2.0 GHz (Intel Core i3 4th gen / AMD A8 or equivalent)
  • Memory: 4 GB RAM
  • Graphics: DirectX 11 compatible graphics; integrated is enough (Intel HD Graphics 4400 or equivalent)
  • DirectX: Version 11
  • Storage: 1 GB available space
  • Additional Notes: Mouse required. Runs in a small window over your desktop. Rare items and cards are Steam inventory items and need a connection to Steam; the rest of the game plays offline.
