Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

This game is a narrative visual novel themed around politics, history, and literature, and contains the following content that may not be suitable for all age groups: Political themes and ideological debate: The story is set during a revolutionary period in a fictional country and involves discussions of adult themes such as regime change, political struggles, arrests, exile, and executions. The narrative explores the relationship between literature and politics, as well as conflicts between different schools of thought. Mild coarse language: Characters occasionally use mild profanity or heated language when emotionally agitated. Character deaths: The plot includes the deaths of certain characters (e.g., from illness, during exile, execution, etc.), but these events are presented solely through textual narration, with no visual depictions of violence or gore. The core experience of the game lies in advancing the story through dialogue choices, allowing players to witness the clashes and developments among different ideologies during a revolutionary era.
