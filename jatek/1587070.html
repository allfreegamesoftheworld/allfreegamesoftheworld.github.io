Röviden a játékról

You get a fruit every fixed hours of game time, each fruit is different it ranges from rarity, fruit and a multiplier to your basket clicking. You can also chop any unwanted fruit into fruit bits and use those to craft another fruit Each fruits and bits can be traded and sold.
