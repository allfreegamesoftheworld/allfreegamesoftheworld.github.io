Röviden a játékról

Requiem of Dread is a single-player Top-down shooter with rogue-lite elements made in a university project. Dive into mysterious dungeons filled with unique monsters and challenging bosses. Shoot your way through waves of enemies, upgrade your equipment and character and travel into the dark.
