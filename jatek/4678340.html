Röviden a játékról

Väinaa is a country of the dead, and you are walking through it. You start on The Pale Shore with nothing. What you find, you keep. What you kill, you take from. The app doesn't need to be open — time away earns experience, jewels, herbs and dungeon keys, and they're waiting when you come back.
