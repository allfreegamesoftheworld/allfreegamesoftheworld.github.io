Röviden a játékról

A 2D pixel art game where you escape crises born in a toxic workplace. With semi-randomized maps and an increasing variety of traps as you progress, players must master movement techniques and carefully plan the use of limited resources to survive. Perfect for fans of Celeste and Getting Over It.
