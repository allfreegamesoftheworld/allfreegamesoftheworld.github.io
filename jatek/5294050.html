Minimális gépigény

Minimum:
  • OS: Windows 10 64-bit (version 21H1 or newer)
  • Processor: Intel Core i3-4130 / AMD FX-4300 or equivalent (SSE2 instruction set required)
  • Memory: 4 GB RAM
  • Graphics: DirectX 11 capable GPU with 1 GB VRAM (Intel HD Graphics 4400, NVIDIA GeForce GT 730, AMD Radeon R7 240 or better)
  • DirectX: Version 11
  • Storage: 1 GB available space
