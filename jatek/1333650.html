Röviden a játékról

Kingdoms In Chaos is an open world faction PVP MMO. Influenced by old school MMO's like Ultima Online. Choose from 3 races and battle for Brittania's riches! Travel anywhere, Hunt, Explore, Quest or form a war party and destroy your enemies. Form a guild and become Lord of a town!
