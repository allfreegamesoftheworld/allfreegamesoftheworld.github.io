Röviden a játékról

Joutsen Ara is an indie short game that participated in the 2021 Good Game Jam. It tells the story of two girls, each from a different side of the world, who are passionate about nature. When a blue macaw takes flight in Brazil and a whooper swan in Finland, both girls decide to chase after them!
