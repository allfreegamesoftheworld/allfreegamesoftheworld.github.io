Röviden a játékról

Dimwarper is an Icy Tower type of platformer, where You play as a mysterious character, who gained an ability to swap between two dimensions. Your task is to climb seven towers, controlled by seven wizards with different powers. This journey will require from You great focus and speed. Good luck!
