Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

This game contains psychological horror, sudden scares, blood imagery, and disturbing depictions of characters and environments, including distorted appearances, imagery of being watched, and scenes that blur reality and hallucination. The story explores altered memories, uncertainty about identity, and psychological distress. Some scenes feature flashing effects, visual distortion, and unsettling text effects. These elements may cause fear or discomfort. Please consider your sensitivity to this content before playing.
