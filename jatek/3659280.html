Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Off the Grid features violence, including futuristic gunfights, blood, and character deaths within a dystopian setting. The story explores mature themes such as social inequality, abuse of power, and surveillance. The game includes mild and veiled nudity. There are no sexual acts or depictions of sexual violence in the game.
