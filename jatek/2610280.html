Röviden a játékról

The heroes of Finland's past must come together to save the land from oblivion in the hands of the sorcerer, Louhi. The game is a non-canonical sequel to The Finnish War and Sotidrokhima, combining hack-n-slash and real-time strategy gameplay from the two titles.
