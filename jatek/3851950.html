Redshift is a short psychological narrative horror game where you close your eyes to reveal a hidden layer of reality, the REDSHIFT. You are a field technician sent to an abandoned research facility. Your objective? Find out why it's begun outputting power, all on its own.

Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Redshift is a psychological horror story experience intended for mature audiences. The game includes the following content: * Intense atmospheric horror and sustained tension. * Depictions of death, disturbing imagery, and implied violence. * Themes of isolation, paranoia, and mental deterioration. * References to a past casualty event. * Brief, abstract visual sequences that may be unsettling or surreal. Due to the game's heavy psychological themes and unnerving tone, it may not be suitable for players sensitive to horror.
