Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Spirit Cleansers depicts gameplay where a priest and a warrior engage in combat with spiritual forces. The warrior will use various weapons like swords, and chains. When a player character is defeated the screen will fade to black, and as the character falls to the ground and a pool of blood appears underneath their body.
