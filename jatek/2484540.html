Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

The game is about a man who fights a human trafficking gang to save a little girl. The themes of sexual violence and exploitation against children are discussed as a part of the investigation of the protagonist to find the little girl. But nothing sexually explicit is shown in the game.

Minimális gépigény

Minimum:
  • Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system
  • OS: Windows 10
  • Processor: 64-bit processor Intel Core i5 8400 OR AMD Ryzen 5 2600
  • Memory: 8 GB RAM
  • Graphics: 3 GB NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 OR AMD Radeon RX 570
  • Storage: 5 GB available space
  • Additional Notes: The game could still work with different systems than the ones mentioned above. But it may not work perfectly.
