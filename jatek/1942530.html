Röviden a játékról

Anointed: DSK is a single player, 3rd person action/adventure game. As David, the player can run, climb, scale walls, swing from ropes and ride a horse on the journey through ancient Judah - using Stealth, Combat and Strategy to fulfill God's plan and save the people of Keilah from the Philistines.
