Röviden a játékról

A tough, 2D, pixel-art platformer. Overcome 8 progressively more difficult Kaizo-style levels, or try and see how fast you can finish an already completed level. On this journey, momentum, jumps, air dashes, and frequent checkpoints are your friends. The world is your enemy.
