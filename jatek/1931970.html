Röviden a játékról

You Deserve a Seat at the Table provides players with the right tools to excel in corporate environments. This 2D Puzzle game offers an exciting alternative to common dull approaches. Ordinary business concepts such as Critical Thinking and Problem Solving are taught through interactive mini-games.
