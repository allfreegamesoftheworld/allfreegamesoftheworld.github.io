Röviden a játékról

Elemates is an action-adventure game about a rebellious girl out to prove her worth in the heroic trials of her tribe. There, she finds an unlikely ally in her grumpy guide. As a team, prevail through the mighty god Quetzal's temple and use the Band of Humility to unleash powerful, elemental spells.
