Röviden a játékról

The Fanciful Chronicle is a singleplayer first person narrative game. As Mike, you find yourself at a highschool reunion where all kind of memories return to you as you meet your friend Sam. As you explore these memories, Mike discovers more and more about his past - or lack thereof.
