Röviden a játékról

Tiny Terrors is a casual 2D action game with a top-down view where you control a tiny ghost and collect candy. On Halloween night, a lonely ghost child encounters a mysterious jack-o'-lantern bucket... A short story presented entirely in hand-drawn animation. Two endings.
