Röviden a játékról

FantasyKingdom is an exciting hidden object 3D puzzle game in a fantasy–medieval setting. There are many items on the location, like numerous trading tents, fortress walls, village houses and other medieval decorations. The player must explore every part of the location to find the necessary items.
