Röviden a játékról

Slimegeon is a 3D third-person roguelike dungeon-crawler game where the player runs through dungeons and possesses defeated enemies to use their abilities and takedown bosses to continue to the next floor. Through the dungeon, the player can level up the unique skill trees for each enemy.
