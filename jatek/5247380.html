Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Memory Wastes contains stylized turn-based combat against human survivors, mutants, zombies and machines, including the use of weapons and references to death, injury, disease, and bones or skeletons. The game includes bleak post-apocalyptic themes and dark humor. It does not contain realistic gore, dismemberment, torture, sexual content, or drug use.
