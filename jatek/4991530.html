Röviden a játékról

Ultimate Dengon Game is an online party game where you place objects instead of drawing. Build a scene from simple shapes, then an opponent changes just one thing to sabotage it. Your teammate must guess the original prompt. A tiny change can turn a serious creation into total nonsense.
