Röviden a játékról

Grim Siege is a 1v1 dark-fantasy card duel. Raise your own tower or tear down your enemy's. Six classes and a deep status system turn every turn into a puzzle of combos and counterplay. Take on the AI or other players in quick matches and ranked battles.

Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Grim Siege is set in a grim, dark-fantasy world. Card artwork depicts fantasy and medieval violence — combat, death and blood. Some illustrations feature characters in revealing outfits with sexualized elements (cleavage, bare midriffs, emphasized sensuality) and partial nudity. The game contains no explicit sexual content.
