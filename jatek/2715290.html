Minimális gépigény

Minimum:
  • OS: Windows 10 or later
  • Processor: Intel i5-4590 / AMD Ryzen 5 1500X or greater
  • Memory: 8 GB RAM
  • Graphics: NVIDIA GeForce GTX 970 / AMD 400 Series or greater. Some specific graphics cards are not supported or recommended. See the Meta website to check for yours.
  • Network: Broadband Internet connection
  • Storage: 2 GB available space
  • VR Support: SteamVR. Meta Quest 2 or 3.
  • Additional Notes: 1x USB port. Oculus PC software needed for Meta Quest Link.
