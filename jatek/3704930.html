Röviden a játékról

BurnerSphere is an immersive social VR experience where players explore VR documentary content filmed at Burning Man. Navigate iconic art installations, engage with interactive workshops, and connect with friends or meet new ones to go on adventures. Experience Burner Culture like never before!
