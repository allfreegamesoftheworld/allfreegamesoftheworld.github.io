Röviden a játékról

Play solo or with up to 4 players in Last Spirit Origin, a fast-paced roguelike adventure. Crash-land on your former home, now an alien-infested world, and battle relentless machine foes. Combine loot to power up and master each character. Use sharp combat and smart strategy to conquer the chaos.
