Röviden a játékról

Brainwave Trials presents itself as a first-person puzzle-platformer experience where the player takes the role of a volunteer within the Garnet Research And Development Sector. By utilizing parkour mechanics and dimensional manipulation, the player will be able to overcome the trials of science.
