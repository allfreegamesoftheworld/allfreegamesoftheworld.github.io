Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

The app itself ships with no mature content only a default hub environment and default avatars. Basis Labs allows connections to independently hosted community servers. There is no central content service: worlds, avatars, and props are created and hosted by users and server operators, and are not pre-reviewed. Some user content may include mature material such as strong language, violence, or nudity/suggestive themes. Creators can tag their content with a content rating, and an in-app content filter can hide content tagged as mature. Tools to mute and block other players and hide their content are built in, and server operators enforce their own rules.
