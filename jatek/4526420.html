Röviden a játékról

Amarta: Kingdom of Shadows is a Kingdom-like game based on the Indonesian Wayang story of Babad Wanamarta, where players build a new nation and kingdom from a forbidden forest. This harmonious kingdom is ruled by the wise leaders, the Pandawa Lima.
