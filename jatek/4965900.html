Röviden a játékról

Crown Marshal is an online medieval fantasy strategy game with turn-based deployment and automatic battles. Recruit your army, shape your formation, read the enemy, and counter their plans. Once battle begins, your forces fight on their own—victory is decided by judgment, not reflexes.
