Röviden a játékról

Dream Channel is a pilot produced in 2016 (being replaced in 2020) for a branching narrative XR story format (linear &amp; non linear) . Audiences choose which way the story goes - resulting in 9 different story endings. Story branches take also the audience into VR mode to reach a particular ending
