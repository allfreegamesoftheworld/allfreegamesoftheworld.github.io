Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

The game does not contain nudity, profanity, or graphic violence. However, the storyline and in-game descriptions explore serious, mature themes. It features motifs such as cultism, highly militarized societies, religious extremism, and complex socio-political issues. Due to the heavy nature of these topics, the game is intended for mature audiences and may not be suitable for younger players.
