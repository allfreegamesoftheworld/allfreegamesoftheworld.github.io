Röviden a játékról

Grindwick is a free-to-play 2D online action RPG built around the grind. Choose an expedition, hunt monsters, find better gear, and grow stronger through combat skills and a passive tree. Play solo or in a party of four. No quests, just the next hunt.

Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Grindwick features stylized pixel-art fantasy combat, visible red blood effects, defeated monsters, and dark dungeon environments. It does not contain graphic dismemberment or sexual content.
