Röviden a játékról

Vartra Abyss is an isometric ARPG that has the player exploring a dystopian underground cavern in search of their lost friend. You must learn to fight using your new abilities that you will attain by slaying enemies. Play your way and mix up your abilities to suit your playstyle.
