Röviden a játékról

Soul Harvest is a fast paced local multiplayer strategy game set in the depths of Hell! Play as Archdemons fighting for the domination of the underworld, harvest souls to summon armies of minions or to cast unholy magicks and send your enemies into oblivion!

Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

The game's pixel-art demons fight and kill one another, so there is pixelated cartoon blood, as well as some bones and dead bodies. Death is not shown in a detailed and graphical way, but it is present, and the Hellish setting may be disturbing to some.
