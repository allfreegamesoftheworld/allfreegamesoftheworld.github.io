Röviden a játékról

Oberak is a third-person Action Adventure RPG and Social School Sim mash-up, set in a Sci-Fantasy world with hip-hop influences. As a powerful Qi warrior, nurture your students, master skills from martial arts to crafting, and defend Ti-an from the deadly rogue clans that are on the rise.
