Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

This game contains surreal and symbolic content that may not be suitable for all audiences. It includes stylized nudity (no genitalia), suggestive humor, unsettling body imagery (such as disembodied figures and self-surgery involving limbs), depictions of a character consuming a baby, references to drug-like experiences, and a storybook sequence with sexually themed and crude language. Please note: the game does not depict or glamorize sexual assault, suicide, or drug use, but it may touch on emotionally intense topics such as grief, isolation, and bodily transformation through symbolic or narrative means.
