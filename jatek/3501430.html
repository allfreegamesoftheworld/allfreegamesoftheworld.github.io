Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

This game contains cartoonish and highly unrealistic themes presented in a humorous, exaggerated manner. Gameplay involves a purely fictional and abstract scenario in which small, clearly non-realistic characters are thrown from a moving train to score points. There is no gore and no depiction of injury or suffering. The visuals are pixelated / low-poly PS1-style, intentionally stylized and detached from realism. All actions are presented as comedic and absurd, without any narrative context meant to represent real-world violence. The content is marked as General Mature Content due to its dark humor and unconventional premise, which may not be suitable for younger audiences, despite the lack of realistic or graphic elements.
