Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Warmonger: 45 is a military-themed first-person shooter featuring large-scale battles between two opposing factions. Players engage in combat using a variety of firearms and explosives in an effort to capture and control objectives. The game includes frequent depictions of combat violence, including gunfire, explosions, and melee attacks. Corpses remain on the battlefield after a player is eliminated, but there is no blood or gore. There are no depictions of self-harm, drug or alcohol use, or sexual content. This game is suitable for players comfortable with military-style first-person shooter gameplay.
