Röviden a játékról

SteamForge is an indie clicker game where you farm diamonds by clicking an anvil. Use your diamonds to open mysterious creatures that drop valuable items. These items can be sold on the Steam Marketplace. Simple, addictive gameplay with endless opportunities for trading and earning.
