Minimális gépigény

Minimum:
  • Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system
  • OS: 64-bit Windows® 10 or later
  • Processor: 64-Bit Dual-Core 2.0 GHz or better, AVX support
  • Memory: 2 GB RAM
  • Graphics: At least 2GB VRAM, OpenGL 4.4 or later
  • Storage: 6 GB available space
  • Additional Notes: We recommend playing on a 16:9 FullHD capable screen at a resolution of 1920x1080. Other resolutions are possible but will introduce letterboxing/pillarboxing to accommodate. Note: there are reports of black screens on the Nvidia GTX 765M and 950M graphics cards due to driver bugs. Please switch to Intel Integrated Graphics if your iGPU supports OpenGL 4.4 or later.
