It is an action-based fighting fun game. Monster Mu throws fruits to you. He throws faster and faster, sometimes throwing multiple fruits at once. Punch the fruit at the right time and finish your fruit cocktail or the fruit will explode in your face. Caution: Don't let the toaster hit you.
