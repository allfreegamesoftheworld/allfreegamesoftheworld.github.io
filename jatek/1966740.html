Röviden a játékról

H.I.V.E. is a roguelite autochess game where the player leads the Clone revolution. It's the year 2424 a technology corporation has created the H.I.V.E. clones to take care of sanitation tasks. But now these beings have gained awareness and are taking over the city. Lead the clones to victory.
