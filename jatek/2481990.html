Röviden a játékról

'Garganta' is a 3D Platformer influenced by Tsutomu Nihei's works. Embody Servo, an android racing to neutralise a device that'll engulf the Universe in innumerable singularities. Encounter peculiar characters and unveil secret plots designed by an AI, maddened by incomprehensible horrors.
