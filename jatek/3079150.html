Röviden a játékról

GuiGui is a clicker game where you click on a cute character named GuiGui! Every 3 hours, you get a random character item from the GuiGui universe. Each item is an original creation by the renowned Korean webtoon artist and oil painter, GuiGui(귀귀). Join us and be part of something fantastic!
