Röviden a játékról

Slacker Squad is a lightweight desktop idle game that reacts to your keyboard and mouse activity. Work hard and your squad speeds up; slack off and they do too. Mine, cook, unlock characters, and capture defeated monsters as pets while your squad keeps you company.
