Röviden a játékról

The Blautopf cave system is one of the most spectacular natural phenomena of Southern Germany. It is only accessible to scientists. With BlautopfVR - The Secrets of Lau you can now explore these hidden places in a Mystery Adventure Game for HTC Vive.Every year, over half a million tourist are drawn to the Blautopf in Blaubeuren.
