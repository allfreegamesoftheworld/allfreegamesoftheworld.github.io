Röviden a játékról

Vahnora is a free-to-play 3D MMORPG of faction war. Choose Sanctum or Forsaken, master one of eleven classes, hunt world bosses for gear, carry relics through enemy lines in Mortis Pass and storm your faction's castle with your guild.
