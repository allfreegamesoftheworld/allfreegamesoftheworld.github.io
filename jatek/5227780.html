Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

This game explores mature psychological themes, including severe depression, mental trauma, and existential crisis. It features dark, liminal environments, clinical psychiatric settings, and the visual depiction of prescription medication (e.g., scattered pills). The game relies heavily on psychological horror and a deeply oppressive atmosphere. There is no physical violence, gore, or sexual content, but the overall experience may be disturbing to sensitive players. Player discretion is advised.
