Röviden a játékról

The Finnish Virtual Art Gallery has a collection of famous Finnish paintings e.g Akseli Gallen-Kallela, Albert Edelfelt, Eero Järnefeld, Ferdinand von Wright, Fanny Churberg, Hugo Simberg and Helene Schjerfbeck.
