Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Astral Court DX has mentions/themes of crime, death-row along with a religious theming - with the game taking place in Heaven and having depictions of angels, demons, Heaven and Hell. The Heaven shown in Astral Court is not intended to be an accurate depiction of Heaven shown in any actual Religious writings, and has no intention to offend any religious person. Astral Court's protagonist is a criminal, but it's never mentioned what crimes they have committed. There's no intention to glorify criminal activity with this character and the game should hopefully reflect that - though there is no preachy "crime is BAD!!!" message either.
