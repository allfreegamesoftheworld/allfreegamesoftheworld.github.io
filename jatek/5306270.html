Röviden a játékról

Stancefolio is an ink-wash wuxia roguelike deckbuilder. Choose one of six martial artists, combine techniques in turn-based battles, and chart your path through encounters and bosses. Upgrade your deck and unlock new cards across runs to discover your own fighting style.
