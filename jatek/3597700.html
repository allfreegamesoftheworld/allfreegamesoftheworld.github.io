Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

This game contains scenes of frequent psychological horror, disturbing imagery, and intense violence. Themes of fear, hallucination, mental instability, and existential dread are explored. There is no depiction of sexual content, drug use, or self-harm. Player discretion is advised.
