Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Sudden Attack Zero Point is a First-Person Shooter, which contains violence involving guns & explosives against other human beings, repetitive yet not too intense. The blood effects displayed when being attacked or attacking an opponent are not realistic but accompany VFX/SFX.
