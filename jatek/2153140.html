Röviden a játékról

Just Another Life is a narrative-driven &quot;life simulation&quot; visual novel mixed with adventure game puzzle elements. Play as John Doe, just another life in the little town of Hope. Help John learn the stories of the various characters of Hope, and help him overcome the hurdles to his own happiness.
