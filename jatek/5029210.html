Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Havfun is a social casino and social world experience intended for adults (18+). Simulated gambling: The game features casino-style games including a slot machine, roulette and blackjack, presented in a casino-themed environment. These games are played exclusively with "Blue Chip", a virtual in-game currency. Blue Chip has no cash value, cannot be redeemed or exchanged for money, cryptocurrency, gift cards, goods or anything of real-world value, and cannot be traded or transferred between players. The game offers no real-money gambling, wagering, cash prizes or cash-out of any kind. Outcomes are for entertainment only. In-game purchases: Cosmetic and other in-game items can be purchased with real money through Steam. Blue Chip is not sold on its own; a bonus amount of Blue Chip is granted alongside certain item purchases. Blue Chip can also be earned for free through minigames and daily in-game activities. Online social features: The game includes shared online spaces with other players and in-game text chat. Player interactions and user-generated content are not fully predictable and may include material that some users find objectionable. A word filter is applied to chat, and players can report other users and block them, which hides the blocked user's messages.
