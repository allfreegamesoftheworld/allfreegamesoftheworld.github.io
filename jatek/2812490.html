Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

In Hunter beat, players will encounter arcade action elements, the main one being shooting cartoon ducks. On a successful hit, the ducks explode, displaying exaggerated animation effects, including the appearance of exaggerated bones and cartoon-style blood. These scenes are intended for comic effect and are designed to be non-realistic. They are not intended to reproduce or glorify actual violence or cruelty. However, we recognize that this stylistic approach may be perceived differently and caution that some players may find these scenes sensitive or unexpected.
