Röviden a játékról

Fight to the beat in a cyberpunk nightclub where movement is your weapon and rhythm is your health. Move, attack, and dash on the beat in this top-down rhythm action roguelike. Chain precise actions, build combos, and survive escalating waves as the music speeds up and the arena grows more chaotic.
