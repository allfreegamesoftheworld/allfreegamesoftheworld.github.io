Röviden a játékról

Dusk Hollow is a horror hack-and-slash game that follows a miner braving the depths of an underground cave system, to rescue his friends who have seemingly been taken by the cave itself. Using what tools he finds around him, the protagonist has to fight against monsters inhabiting the cavern.
