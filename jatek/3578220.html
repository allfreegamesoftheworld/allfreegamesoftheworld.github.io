Röviden a játékról

Doors is a short 3D sci-fi puzzle game. You and your army of clones move in unison to escape the labyrinth one puzzle at a time. Flip levers, open doors, reach high places, see from new perspectives, create clones, swap with them and destroy them to solve puzzles in a desolate landscape.
