Röviden a játékról

Spooky Night is an online social deduction game of teamwork and betrayal! Uncover the werewolves among you before they wipe out the village. During the day, chat, deceive, and vote to eliminate a suspect. At night, use your role’s abilities to gather insights or strike. Can you survive?
