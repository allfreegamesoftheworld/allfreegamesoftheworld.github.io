Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Drugrunners is a fictional, text-based crime simulation game centered on drug trafficking and organized crime themes. Players run cartels, deal in fictional narcotics, and compete against rival cartels. No real-world controlled substances are depicted, glorified, or sold; the "drugs" are abstract in-game commodities represented as text and numbers, not imagery. The game also includes strong crime-themed language and simulated violence between rival players/cartels.
