Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Slapstick cartoon violence: sheep explode, fall, drown, burn, get eaten by a wolf or end up in a meat grinder, with exaggerated cartoon blood and bones. Blood can be switched off in the settings (it turns into wool). Dark humor about death, with a sausage factory, hell and Satan.
