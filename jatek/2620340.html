Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

In Bounty City, players engage in thrilling shootouts using various weapons. We understand the importance of responsible gaming, and we want to assure you that while the game does involve shooting, the graphic content is designed to be more on the arcade side rather than gory or overly realistic. Our focus is on providing an exciting and immersive gaming experience without crossing into overly mature or graphic territory. We believe in striking a balance between engaging gameplay and respectful content, ensuring players can enjoy the game without encountering excessively mature themes. If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out. We're here to make sure all our players have an enjoyable and comfortable gaming experience.
