Röviden a játékról

A small, third-person, action, tower defense game where you play as a peasant who gets tired of adventurers breaking into his house. Place down towers and fight off waves and waves of &quot;enemies.&quot; Progress through the story and see just how far one man will go to protect his beloved llama.
