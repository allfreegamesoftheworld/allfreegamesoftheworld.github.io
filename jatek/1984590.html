Röviden a játékról

The player, Tal, has been issued a combat challenge. Participate in a one-on-one duel to the death in order to secure your team’s position in the Tyrning. Using artifacts secured during previous hunts, engage in melee combat with the opposing team’s champion. Made by four Drury University students.
