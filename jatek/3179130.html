Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Our game features cartoonish, low-poly violence where the player, as a rooster, uses guns and explosives to defeat humanoid animals (foxes, wolves, etc.). There are red blood splashes when characters are shot. The game also includes suggestive humor, with the title 'PlayCOQ' being a playful reference. The visual style uses a yolk-and-eggwhite design for the lettering, which might insinuate adult themes without any explicit sexual content.
