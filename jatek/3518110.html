Röviden a játékról

Exit the Abyss is a psychological horror game where you are trapped in an abandoned, haunted hospital. The game focuses on an oppressive atmosphere, exploration, and mysteries, with minimalist puzzle elements. Immerse yourself in a distorted reality, confront your fears, and find the way out!

Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

The game contains strong psychological horror elements, including a disturbing atmosphere, jump scares, and stress-inducing sound effects. The story revolves around a state between life and death, which may cause anxiety and discomfort for sensitive players. The game does not contain extreme violence, gore, sexual content, or scenes depicting self-harm.
