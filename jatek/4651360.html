Röviden a játékról

Set in the futuristic city of Neo-Santara in the year 2065, 'Enter the Glampong' offers a new way to explore and appreciate street art in a digital space. The environment is a full-scale recreation of Singapore’s historic Kampong Glam neighborhood, reimagined as a vibrant street art district.
