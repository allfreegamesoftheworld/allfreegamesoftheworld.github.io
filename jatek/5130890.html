Röviden a játékról

&quot;Parasomnia&quot; is a 2D pixel-style psychological horror game. Players will delve into the inner world of a child named Sunny who suffers from &quot;night terrors&quot;. Through the perspective of Sunny, they will experience the fear and struggles in her subconscious.
