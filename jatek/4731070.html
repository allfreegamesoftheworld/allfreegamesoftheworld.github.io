Röviden a játékról

A persistent space MMO that runs in your browser. No download. No installation. Open a tab and you are in the Frontier. Trade, mine and fight across 2 universes and 12,000 sectors, in an economy that players build. And one mechanic that no other MMO has: war rewrites the map.

Minimális gépigény

Minimum:
  • Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system
  • OS: Windows 10
  • Processor: Dual-core 1.8 GHz or equivalent
  • Memory: 2048 MB RAM
  • Graphics: Any integrated graphics (DirectX 9 compatible)
  • Network: Broadband Internet connection
  • Storage: 100 MB available space
  • Sound Card: N/A
  • Additional Notes: Kron Wars is a browser-based MMO — no installation required. Play via any modern web browser; all game content is server-side. A stable broadband connection of 10 Mbps or faster is recommended for the best experience in real-time trading, galactic wars, and PvP/PvE combat.
