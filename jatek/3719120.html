Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Our game centers on an arcane cult that aims to resurrect an ancient deity. To accomplish this, they have kidnapped the protagonist. Their plan is to sacrifice him to summon a god who will unleash chaos and terror upon the Earth. The game features dark, eerie locations, mysterious figures whose state—alive or dead—is uncertain, enemies with features resembling cadaverous birds, and an environmental narrative in which stains and filth create a disturbing atmosphere. You will shoot your crossbow against the enemies, who will fall dead and disappear. And once you pick up the doomed dagger, you will hear unsettling whispers.
