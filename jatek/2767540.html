Röviden a játékról

Ball Bulét is an isometric, Bullet Hell, twin-stick shooter in which a girl searches for revenge by fighting security guards with...a bouncing ball?! Progress through the building by performing ricochets with your spherical weapon against walls and enemies to reach the top.
