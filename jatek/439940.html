Lucid9 is a visual novel focused on a web of intricate mysteries set in the fictional 2018 metropolis of Isamu. Lucid9: Inciting Incident is the first part of the story, focused on a string of disappearances that occur at a prestigious, uppity boarding academy.

Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

This visual novel contains mature themes and content, including psychological horror, violence, blood, strong language, and discussions of mental health issues including suicide. Characters engage in intense emotional confrontations and there are scenes depicting physical altercations, injury, and death. The story explores dark topics such as murder, trauma, and moral ambiguity. References to drug and alcohol use appear throughout, including depictions of substance abuse and its consequences. While there is no explicit sexual content, there are some suggestive themes and references. The game features occasional use of profanity and crude humor. Due to these elements, this title is recommended for mature audiences who are comfortable with psychological thriller content and heavy narrative themes.
