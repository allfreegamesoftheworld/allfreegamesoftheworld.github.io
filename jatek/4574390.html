Röviden a játékról

Experience the thrill of VR bowling! Strike Again VR is a bowling roguelike game. Select an initial loadout and bowl through the 21 antes, while upgrading along the way. With 4 ball types, and 3 pin types with many different stacks, there's plenty of variety! Can you bowl up to the challenge?
