Röviden a játékról

Enter the arena in PKWAR, a hardcore PvP MUD where text-based combat meets competitive glory. Choose from 5 unique classes, battle players simultaneously, and rise through military ranks from Private to Captain. No graphics, no distractions - just pure skill-based combat. Death or glory.

Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

This is an online player-killing mud (multi user dungeon). You will obtain weapons and unique items to meet new people, and hunt them down and kill them.
