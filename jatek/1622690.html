Röviden a játékról

Appetite for Detestation is a reverse-horror action platformer where you take the role of a monster awoken from a stone statue. Embark on a bloodthirsty rampage through randomly generated selection of a fictional Victorian era city locations into the Church to satiate your hunger with holy blood.
