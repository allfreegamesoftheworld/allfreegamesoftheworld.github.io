Röviden a játékról

The Twilight Witch is a 2D pixel art RPG where you play as Mael, a young man from the village of New Larton. After your home-town is attacked and ravaged by a mysterious figure, you venture forth to tell of what happened and maybe, just maybe, find a way to stop the hooded aggressor…
