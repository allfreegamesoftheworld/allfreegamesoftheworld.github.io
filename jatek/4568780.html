Vertal Strike is a free-to-play multiplayer arena FPS with rapid respawns, Team Deathmatch and Free-for-All. Build a kill streak in Quick Play, trigger the siren hunt and survive the chase.

Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Vertal Strike contains competitive first-person shooter combat with firearms, explosives, player deaths, and a self-elimination mechanic. Violence is presented in a stylized way without graphic gore or sexual content.
