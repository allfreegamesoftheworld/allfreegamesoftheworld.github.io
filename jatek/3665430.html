Röviden a játékról

Kaporanga is a local and online party game for up to 8 players of all ages, packed with mini-games set across iconic places in Rio de Janeiro. It’s free and accessible! Grab your friends, cousins, even the parrot — and get ready for some wild fun and friendly chaos. Time to Kaporanga!
