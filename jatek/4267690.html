Röviden a játékról

RK Override is an hour-long fully voiced interactive narrative following Chimera, a Mecha Captain fighting in an endless war for their country’s future. Fight for control against your Mecha’s AI copilot (RK System) as you attempt to end the conflict - no matter the cost.
