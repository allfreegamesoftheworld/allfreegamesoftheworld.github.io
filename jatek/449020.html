Röviden a játékról

Play as Alice who has just had an unsettingly life-like dream about the end of the world, and is retelling it to her friend. A single play-through might take as little as 20 minutes, but experiencing all the possible paths Alice can take through the ruins of her city will take you many hours.

Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Contains descriptions of the effects of nuclear war on the human body and society, but all descriptions are flltered through a conversation, and is generally not very explicit. There are no images of gore or violence (some of the images depict ruins, but no bodies or blood).
