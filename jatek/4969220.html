Röviden a játékról

Basil &amp; Za'atar is a warm, cooperative, puzzle-driven story of a boy and a girl who live on opposing sides of the Mediterranean. Grab a friend and explore your differences together. Your success relies on you communicating what you see... and really listening for what you don’t.
