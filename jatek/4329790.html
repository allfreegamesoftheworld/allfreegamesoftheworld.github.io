Röviden a játékról

Cave Crashers is a top-down arcade shooter game where two players take control of star-nosed moles, and cooperate to pilot a mining rig through bug infested caves. Progress as far as you can, collect valuable shards to increase your score while defending your RIG from bugs using your turrets.
