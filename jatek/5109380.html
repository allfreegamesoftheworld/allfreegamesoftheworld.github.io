Röviden a játékról

A Decent Farewell is a short narrative VR experience that blends psychological horror with an emotional story about memory, loss, and saying goodbye. Explore an unsettling environment, interact with objects, and uncover the meaning behind a deeply personal farewell.

Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

This game contains mature themes related to death, funerals, grief, and loss. Players may encounter depictions of deceased human bodies in a mortuary or funeral setting, along with unsettling imagery and psychological horror elements. There is no sexual content or nudity. Any depiction of blood or injury is limited and non-graphic.
