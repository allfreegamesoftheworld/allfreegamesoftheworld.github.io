Röviden a játékról

Controller is a light Tower Control simulator. Use a simplified instruction system to manage movements of planes around an Airfield. Dynamic hazards will keep on you your toes and force you to alter your plans on the fly. Do you have what it takes to manage the busiest airports in the world?
