Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

The story in vivid/stasis covers the following topics that some may preferred to be warned about beforehand: - Characters frequently swear both in text messages and out loud with other characters - The story generally covers themes of unreality, trauma, and poor mental health - The game makes use of its status as a video game as a tool for its story - Some story content covers themes of survivors' guilt, grief and mourning
