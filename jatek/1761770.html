Röviden a játékról

Universe 51: Tannhäuser Wars is a free-to-play fast-paced multiplayer FPS, taking inspiration from games like DOOM and Quake. In Tannhäuser Wars, two unfamiliar groups are mysteriously teleported to the same location. Since communication between them is impossible or undesirable, they clash!
