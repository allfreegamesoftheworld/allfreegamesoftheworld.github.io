Röviden a játékról

The Tower is a casual-action style game with various levels and a final boss level. In this game, one must find or craft new weapons to defeat your foes, the chicken army. Along with enemy chickens, the Chicken Elder has also set up various traps and obstacles that block your way to him. Good luck!
