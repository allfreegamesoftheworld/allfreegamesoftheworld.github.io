Röviden a játékról

Bomber 95 is the recreation of old school bomberman like game. With the ability to play with friends on one PC or over the Internet or both of those simultaneously. Nine unique levels with their own features, a lot of blow-ups and power-ups that can change gameplay balance instantly.
