Röviden a játékról

Critterland is a fast-paced 2D real-time strategy (RTS) game that pits you against the forces of nature. Solve puzzles in the campaign, discovering synergies between species while enjoying the atmospheric OST. Alternatively, conduct your own ecological research in sandbox mode.
