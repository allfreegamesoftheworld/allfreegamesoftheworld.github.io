Röviden a játékról

The pinnacle of CowGame technology. Cow Escape is a micro scale action game set in a dystopian near future. Play as a cow in the resistance against the meat industry. Fight to free calves from the slaughterhouse, survive endless waves in the rodeo and utilize your powers to destroy Cowmen thugs.
