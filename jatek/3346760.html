Röviden a játékról

Whisperstring is an 80s inspired text adventure game that takes place inside a “chat room”. You’re connected to Adam, a &quot;jock&quot; with a severe fear of strings, who seems to have been the victim of a supernatural kidnapping. Will you be able to help him escape?
