Röviden a játékról

Bird Game: Tokyo is a fast-paced multiplayer flight game where you soar above sprawling cityscapes, weave between buildings, compete with other birds. Choose your bird, use its unique strengths, manage your energy, and outfly your opponents. Bird Game: Tokyo is a game about birds... in Tokyo.
