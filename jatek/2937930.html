Röviden a játékról

Deadlands Duel is a wave-based zombie defense shooter that takes place throughout all of history via time travel. You will fight hoards of zombies with various weapons and armaments. Choose one of four class types to specialize your gameplay to fit your desired role in this team-play focused arena.
