Röviden a játékról

Dark rooms, difficult puzzles, and enemies popping out of the dark, Lights Out VR is about traversing through an escape room environment where each room becomes harder than the last. Players will be allowed to run, jump, teleport, whatever gets you from point A to point B, so long as you escape.
