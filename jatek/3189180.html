Röviden a játékról

Anima is a puzzle-platformer in mysterious technological surroundings. Use your controller to move and deconstruct obstacles in an abandoned mining station through two of its robots, Min.I and Andro.ID. Make a path to your goals, run, jump and bounce to discover what became of your creators!
