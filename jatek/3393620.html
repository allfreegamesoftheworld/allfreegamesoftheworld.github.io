Röviden a játékról

Double Shoulders is a two player action-adventure game. You take control of Lois and Sam, two kids trying to escape from a strange place filled with dangers and challenges. You'll have to work together to get out of this place alive.

Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Double Shoulders contains violent elements that primarily focus on the main characters, who are children. While their deaths can be brutal and violent, the game does not feature grotesque gore or dismemberment. The violent scenes are suggestive and serve the story, without aiming to shock or provoke excessively. It is important to note that players may encounter moments of tension and emotional distress that contribute to the dark and oppressive atmosphere of the game. However, no explicitly shocking content such as sexual assaults, alcohol or drug abuse, or self-harm is present. We advise players sensitive to such content to consider this approach before embarking on the adventure.
