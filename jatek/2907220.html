Röviden a játékról

Steel Symphony is a 2D Action-Strategy platformer where players craft combat sentinels by selecting components and balancing stats like integrity, mobility, and weapon capabilities. Chassis, weapons, and modules shape each sentinel's role with varied abilities for strategic customization.
