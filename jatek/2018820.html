Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

ELVERA is an Action RPG that includes combat scenarios and battles involving both melee and ranged weapons. While the game is not overly gory or violent, it does feature fantasy combat elements, such as the use of swords, staffs, and bows. There is no depiction of sexual content, explicit or otherwise, in the game. Nor does it contain elements of drug or alcohol abuse, self-harm, or any sensitive social themes. Players should expect to engage in fantasy violence but can rest assured that the game is designed with a broad audience in mind and does not delve into explicit or overly sensitive topics.
