Röviden a játékról

Mauled is a Free-To-Play indie TPS by a solo developer. Fight relentless hordes alone or in online Co-op across varied maps. Collect tiered weapons, trigger tactical traps, and upgrade your character's stats as the difficulty rises round after round. Survival is your only goal.
