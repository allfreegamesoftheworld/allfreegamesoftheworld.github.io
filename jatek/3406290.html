The Forgotten Village is a Fantasy Simulation RPG. Arrive in a rundown Village with nothing but the shirt on your back. Clear the land, Gather Resources, Help the Villagers and Earn new Tools to aid you in restoring the Forgotten Village back to its former Glory!
