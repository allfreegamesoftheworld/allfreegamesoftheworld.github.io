Röviden a játékról

Lamplight is a deckbuilding turn based roguelike RPG set in Victorian era England. Van Helsing is dead, Dracula has cloaked the old world in darkness, there is only one hope that remains. Take control of one of four characters from classical gothic literature and fight to kill him once and for all.

Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

This game doesn't contain a lot of traditional gore but it does contain large amount of blood and bleeding. Their are depictions of nooses but these are in the context of state execution and not self harm.
