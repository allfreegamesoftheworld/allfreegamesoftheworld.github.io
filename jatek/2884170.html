Röviden a játékról

Dive into HyperBody dungeons: an experimental VR walking simulator blending architectural ruins, cult themes, sensorial vibes, and queer narratives. Navigate mysterious realms and affective tales, and immerse in a psychedelic, rhythm-fueled cosmos. Experience a meditative, aestheticised trance!

Minimális gépigény

Minimum:
  • Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system
  • OS: Windows 10 (64-bit)
  • Processor: Intel Core i5-7500 / Ryzen 5 1600
  • Memory: 16 GB RAM
  • Graphics: GeForce GTX 1080 Ti
  • DirectX: Version 11
  • Storage: 30 GB available space
  • VR Support: OpenXR, Seated with Swivel Chair, Standing, Roomscale. Valve Index, HTC Vives and Pico using SteamVR. Oculus headsets with Oculus Runtime. WMR headsets with WMR Runtime.
  • Additional Notes: Virtual Desktop not recommended (Questlink/Airlink Fully Supported)
