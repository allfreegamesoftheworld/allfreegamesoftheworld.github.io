Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

The game features horror elements designed to create a dark and unsettling atmosphere, primarily relying on shock moments. Players may encounter occasional depictions of blood, and there are 'creatures' that can kill characters, but these encounters are not depicted in a brutally graphic way. The emphasis is on suspense and surprise rather than explicit violence. Players should be aware that the themes and sudden frights may be unsettling for some.
