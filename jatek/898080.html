Interact with sad, alive objects! Throw away a rotten apple; pet a stubborn cactus; sit on a grumpy chair; bond with your pet roomba. Explore your studio apartment, discover how to set boundaries, and use the power of saying &quot;no&quot; to discover happiness!

Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

everybody's sad contains themes of depression and contains an easter-egg mechanic that might not be suitable for all ages. Specifically, it contains a vibrator and when the player uses it, it makes them happy.
