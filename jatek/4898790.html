Röviden a játékról

Crosstalk is a hardware architecture design sandbox with a built-in, fully inspectable simulator and emulator. Players can build designs ranging from LED controllers to complex computers using SystemVerilog, then program them. It has a gate-level simulator, introspection tools, a compiler, and more.
