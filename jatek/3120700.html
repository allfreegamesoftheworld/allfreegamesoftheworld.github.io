Röviden a játékról

Farming is a Trading Card Game (TCG). Every 2 hours of online planting will yield a basic crop skin. There is a 30% chance to also yield a rare crop skin each time you yield. In this game you can enhance the level of your crop skins to improve your collection, and join us to make your farm unique!
