Röviden a játékról

A free-to-play sci-fi action roguelike game set in an alien world where the sun never shines. Explore the world as a ‘Planet Adventurer’. Fight hostile creatures, gather ore, and interact with the local community in your downtime. Upgrade your gear with resources earned during your expeditions.
