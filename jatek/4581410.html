Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

This title is an interactive horror-comedy adventure. It contains elements that may not be suitable for all ages, including frequent strong language and dark humor. Although there is no explicit graphic violence, the game features monstrous creatures, zombies, bloodstains, and scenes where characters die (often off-screen, but with audible screams) if the player makes incorrect choices. There are no depictions of sexual acts or substance abuse.
