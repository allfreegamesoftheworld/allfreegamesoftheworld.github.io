Röviden a játékról

Experience the town of Nya Lödöse in the early 16th century! Meet the merchant, the gravedigger, the herbalist and the other town residents and help them in three quests! The game is based on knowledge that has been developed through archaeological investigations in Gamlestaden, Gothenburg.
