Röviden a játékról

In 'The Visitor' you will play as an author named Rachel. Your goal is to complete your book chapter by chapter before the deadline that is coming up. The longer you sit and type, the more you will slip into a schizophrenia state of mind. Complete the chapters and return to a normal average life!
