Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

StoryForge is a text-based narrative experience, and its content is generated as you read, so what appears depends on the genre you choose and the choices you make. Adventure sagas contain fantasy violence and peril, including combat and descriptions of injury or death told in prose. Romance sagas contain romantic and non-explicit sexual themes; intimacy may be implied but is never depicted explicitly. Content across genres may include mild language and references to alcohol in keeping with its settings. StoryForge does not contain explicit sexual content, graphic gore, depictions of sexual assault or non-consensual situations, self-harm, or substance abuse as subject matter. Story generation runs within safety guardrails designed to keep content within the bounds of literary fiction.
