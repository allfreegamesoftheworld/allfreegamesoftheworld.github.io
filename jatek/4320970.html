Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

STIGMA features high-quality 2D illustrations and animations of female characters. Some characters are depicted in revealing, tight-fitting tactical outfits such as high-cut leotards and bodysuits, with partial nudity and suggestive posing. The game also contains stylized fantasy violence as players engage in rhythmic combat against digital entities known as "Noise." The game does not contain explicit sexual acts, interactive sexual content, full nudity, depictions of sexual assault or non-consensual sex, drug or alcohol abuse, self-harm, or graphic gore.
