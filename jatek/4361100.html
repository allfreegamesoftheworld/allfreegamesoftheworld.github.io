Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

This game contains depictions of violence and horror themes consistent with a dark supernatural narrative. Players may encounter ritualistic imagery, references to human sacrifice, blood effects, disturbing environments, and intense scenes related to occult practices. The game does not contain sexual content, sexual assault, nudity, or non-consensual sexual acts. There are no depictions of drug use or self-harm. Some themes may be unsettling for sensitive players, including religious horror, possession, and psychological tension.
