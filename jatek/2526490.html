Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

This game is set in the Middle Ages, where the player takes on the role of a noble. The player engages in raids and sieges where people and horses can die of the weapons from the era. The game shows blood effects and death animations. Player's character can consume beer and wine. Texts from the game contain moderately vulgar languages and archaic forms of curses such as bastard, bloody hell, sard, etc.
