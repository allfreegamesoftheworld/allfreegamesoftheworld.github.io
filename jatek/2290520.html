Röviden a játékról

Sneaky Pirates is a fast-paced online multiplayer social deduction game for 4 players. Hide among ships, steal from rivals, and use power-ups to escape or fight back. Play it safe or go all-in—no winner is safe until the last second! Customize your ship and be the king of pirates! 🏴‍☠️
