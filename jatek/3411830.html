Cards, the Universe and Everything is a unique trading card game with almost limitless possibilities: Dinosaurs, Norse gods, blue-footed boobies, Samurai, volcanoes, even the god particle. With the universe’s thingamabobs and endless strategies, are you ready for this gloriously bonkers game?

Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

What can we say, reality gets a little mature sometimes. Everything in CUE is based on real-world history, science, art, and myth. If you can’t hack Chronos’ penchant for baby-eating (in text form), this probably isn’t the game for you.
