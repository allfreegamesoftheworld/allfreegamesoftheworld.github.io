Röviden a játékról

Don't Give Up is an incremental, minimalist and emotional game about loss. As you gain more passions, friends and dreams they become harder to manage. Each one ignoring your weakening cries as they slowly leave your circle and life. Is it possible to stop everything you love from leaving you?
