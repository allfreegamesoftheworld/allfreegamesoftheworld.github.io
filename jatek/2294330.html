Röviden a játékról

LastRo is a top-down shooter roguelike where you play robot, which mission is to clear up a lot of floors against different enemies. Your enemies are &quot;evolving&quot;, which means they become stronger with each floor and difficulty you beat. Try out different weapons, abilities and upgrades to beat them.
