Röviden a játékról

An online RPG on the island of Moruah. Catch dinos and raise them as pets. Your pet attacks whatever you attack, and Auto Battle keeps the hunt going. Pets raised from Lv 1 grow strongest, and an appraisal reveals their Bloodline. Party up with other hunters on one island. In English and Korean.
