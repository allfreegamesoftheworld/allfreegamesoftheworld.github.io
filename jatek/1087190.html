UNDERTURE is a short 6DoF immersive VR experience set in the New York City subway. Multi-axis freedom of movement, combined with spatialized audio, creates a uniquely immersive and transporting experience for the viewer.

Minimális gépigény

Minimum:
  • OS: 7
  • Processor: Intel i5-4590 or AMD FX 8350 equivalent
  • Memory: 8 GB RAM
  • Graphics: Nvidia GeForce GTX 970 or AMD Radeon R9 290 equivalent
  • Storage: 541 MB available space
  • VR Support: SteamVR or Oculus PC. Keyboard and mouse required
  • Additional Notes: This experience requires a VR head-mounted display. No input after launch (including keyboard/mouse). Ensure proper play space calibration before launching.
