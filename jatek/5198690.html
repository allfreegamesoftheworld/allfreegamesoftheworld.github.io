Röviden a játékról

Balance Due is a solo and online co-op fantasy action game where you hunt monsters, gather loot, upgrade weapons, and return to Base Camp before time runs out. Meet each day’s gold quota, manage the shifting Due, and survive long enough to determine your ending.
