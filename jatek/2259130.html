Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Although the game takes place during World War 2, the presented content does not explicitly show blood/misshapen bodies or close-ups of gory visuals. Gameplay / Cinematics might involve: 1. Dry hits from one human to another 2. dog biting a human 3. Tense, dramatic situations like a kid running from danger. In specific gameplay events, viewed from the side and from a distance, a human is shot in the torso, but no blood is shown.
