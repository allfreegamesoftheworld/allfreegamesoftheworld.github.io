Röviden a játékról

Bang! Howdy is an online strategy game. The setting mixes elements from a variety of related genres, such as steampunk or Native American myth. Several of the players' available units are entirely mechanical, including artillery and a &quot;steam gunman&quot;, a robot boasting large wagon wheels for movement while wearing a cowboy hat.
