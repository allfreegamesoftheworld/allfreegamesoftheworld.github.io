Röviden a játékról

Robo Rig is a 3D, top down, Extraction Bullet Hell where you play as a fully customizable robot. You build your robot, enter battle and destroy enemies, gain coins and collect parts, then you must extract and build an even more powerful robot with the newly acquired parts!
