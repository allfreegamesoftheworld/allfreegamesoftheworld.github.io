Röviden a játékról

High Wizard Clayton has animated clay pots to attack people! Do you have what it takes to stop him? Travel across different worlds fighting High Wizard Clayton's pot army, discovering hidden pots and finding new weapons! Terracotta is a First Person Collectathon. Now go shatter some pots!
