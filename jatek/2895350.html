Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Note that this game features realistic portrayals of weapons and involves shooting targets, including zombies, with blood effects when shot. As a tactical FPS shooter, players will engage in shooting using a variety of firearms. The game aims to provide an immersive experience simulating tactical training scenarios. Players should be aware of the content and nature of the game, including the depiction of violence and gore, before playing.
