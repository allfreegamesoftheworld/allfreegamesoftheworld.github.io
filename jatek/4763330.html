Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

This game is an AI-driven text adventure RPG with no pre-written scripts — all content is generated in real time. Story genres include dark fantasy, historical warfare, and cyberpunk. Narratives may involve combat, organized crime, and political intrigue. NPC dialogue may contain mild profanity. The game does NOT contain sexual violence, sexualized minors, self-harm, or substance abuse content. All player input passes through server-side multi-layer moderation to ensure prohibited content never reaches the AI model.
