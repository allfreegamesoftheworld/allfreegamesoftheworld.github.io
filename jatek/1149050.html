Röviden a játékról

Slime Jump is a 2D precision platformer game where you explore a world full of hazards and can collect optional gems. There are 7 areas to traverse, two difficulties, cosmetics, and achievements. A grappling hook and blaster gun are needed for later parts. Can you rescue and reunite with your lover?
