Röviden a játékról

WarDeck is a turn-based strategy game combining board game tactics with deckbuilding mechanics. Build your unique elemental deck and position your cards wisely on the board. Defeat Bosses in the challenging Adventure mode or challenge rivals in the PvP arena.
