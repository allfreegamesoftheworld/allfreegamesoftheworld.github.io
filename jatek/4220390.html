Röviden a játékról

Cubettiny is a fast-paced 2.5D arcade game where you guide a sad blue cube through low-poly levels filled with obstacles, traps, and power-ups. Collect cubes, complete chains, unlock hidden paths, use power-ups, and push through each stage to bring order back to Numeria!
