Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

This game features physical and gun violence, including melee combat and ranged attacks. Players can engage in fistfights, use melee weapons such as rocks, knives, and grenades, or shoot enemies with firearms. When struck, enemies may stumble and react with pain or collapse into ragdoll physics upon death. Firearm impacts result in blood spray effects and bloody bullet holes, but there is no intense gore—no dismemberment, blood puddles, or smears. Explosions from grenades also cause harm to characters. The game includes mild profanity such as "dammit" and "hell," but no explicit language. There is no sexual content. A "Healing Syringe" item is used to restore health through an injection animation, though this is not explicitly identified as a drug in the game.
