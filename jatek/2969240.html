Röviden a játékról

Shepherds is a 3D, two-player co-op musical adventure game in which players control unlikely partners - a shepherd girl named Zoe and a magic wolf named Kin. Players must cleverly combining their unique mastery of musical melodies and distinctive movement styles to solve puzzles.
