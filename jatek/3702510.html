Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Biophobia explores themes of trauma, death, and the human body through a psychological horror lens. The game contains unsettling imagery, including depictions of corpses, dissection, body horror, and medical environments. Narrative elements touch on topics such as sexism, loss, and emotional distress. While there are no explicit depictions of sexual acts, the game does include references to gender-based violence and oppressive cultural views surrounding women's bodies. There are no portrayals of sexual assault, drug/alcohol abuse, or self-harm. However, players may encounter disturbing scenes related to death, vulnerability, and themes of control over the body. Player discretion is advised.
