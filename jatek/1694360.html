Röviden a játékról

The game tells the story of Jeff Logar and his daughters Tahoma and Sahale. Both girls run away after heavy row with their brutish father Jeff. They got lost in the dark forests of Seattle (USA). There, Jeff will undergo a lifechanging transformation to save his daughters.
