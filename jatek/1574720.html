Röviden a játékról

Diorama Tower Defense: Tiny Kingdom (Prologue) is a cute tower defense game with currently three different dioramas, as well as three difficulty levels. The objective is to stop the enchanted creatures from reaching the king of Tiny Kingdom, since he is allergic to those creatures.
