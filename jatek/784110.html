Röviden a játékról

bLogic Blox is an electronic sandbox where you can create anything your mind can dream up. Build circuit logic with switches, gates, lights, batteries, or just make cool 3D pixel art. Add input bits linked to your keyboard to make interactive objects that you can control. The only limitation is your imagination!
