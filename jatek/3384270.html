Röviden a játékról

Deep Siege is a fast-paced, first-person endless wave shooter. Play as Akula, an Atlantean sharkfolk warrior, and defend your underwater realm against waves of mutated sea creatures. Rack up kills to gain buffs using Bloodthirst or heal during tough moments.
