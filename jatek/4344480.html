Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

This game contains narrative and symbolic references to sensitive themes including domestic violence, gender-based violence, psychological abuse, sexual abuse, postpartum depression, and mental health struggles. These topics are presented through environmental storytelling, symbolism, and narrative interpretation rather than explicit visual depiction. The game does not show sexual acts or explicit violence on screen; instead, it explores the emotional and psychological impact of trauma through atmosphere, dialogue, and environmental cues. Some players may find these themes distressing or triggering. Player discretion is advised.
