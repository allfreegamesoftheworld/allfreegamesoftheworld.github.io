Röviden a játékról

“CATpillAr” is a horror-themed VR mini-game that requires you to collect big, juicy, hairy, creepy caterpillars crawling around an empty hospital. In VR, you can grab harmless giant caterpillars by the neck and throw them inside the bucket. Replay and see how many you can collect!
