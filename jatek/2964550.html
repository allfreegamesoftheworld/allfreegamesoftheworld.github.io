Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

VR Realms is a social platform where players build and share their own worlds and avatars, and talk to each other in live voice and text chat. Content you may encounter includes: - Horror worlds with frightening imagery, jump scares, and psychological themes - Stylized and realistic violence, including blood and depictions of weapons - Strong language - Social spaces that depict alcohol, such as bars - Avatars wearing revealing outfits, and sexual innuendo or sex-related language between players There is no nudity, no explicit sexual content, and no depiction of sexual acts. Community submissions marked as mature are hidden by default and are only visible to players who install the free Mature Content DLC. Conversation between players is not screened in advance, so you may encounter language or behavior from other people that we have not reviewed. Any world, avatar, or player can be reported from the in-game menu.

Minimális gépigény

Minimum:
  • Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system
  • OS: Windows 10 64-bit
  • Processor: Intel Core i5-8400 / AMD Ryzen 5 2600
  • Memory: 8 GB RAM
  • Graphics: NVIDIA GTX 1060 6GB / AMD RX 580 8GB
  • DirectX: Version 12
  • Network: Broadband Internet connection
  • Storage: 10 GB available space
  • VR Support: Meta Quest 2 / 3 / 3S via Link or Air Link, Valve Index, HTC Vive, and other OpenXR / SteamVR headsets. Headset optional, also plays on desktop.
  • Additional Notes: Desktop/flatscreen mode. Verified running on Steam Deck. A VR headset is not required to play.
