Röviden a játékról

Wordcaster is a voice-driven RPG adventure where you become a wizard, casting spells by speaking words aloud. Real-time speech recognition turns your voice into magical attacks against enemies. Unlock spellbooks, battle bosses, collect coins, and level up in a unique voice-powered RPG experience.
