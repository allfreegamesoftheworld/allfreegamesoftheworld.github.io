Röviden a játékról

DEEP 8 is a gritty RPG inspired by 90s classics. Fight tactical battles! Use mods instead of weapons which you can change any time while fighting. Combine them to build effective strategies and crush your enemies! Explore a vast scifi world and uncover the dark past of your characters.
