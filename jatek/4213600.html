Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

This game contains mature psychological themes and follows a female protagonist navigating distressing situations. Players should be aware that the narrative includes direct depictions of stalking and drug abuse. Additionally, there are thematic elements that strongly suggest or imply self-harm, though it is not graphically depicted. To clarify for sensitive audiences: This game DOES NOT contain depictions of sexual assault, non-consensual sex, or BDSM
