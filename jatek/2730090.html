Röviden a játékról

Furballer is an arcade game where you take on the role of a puppet football player. Navigate the stage, kicking the ball into the enemy puppets to rack up points and secure the best show. The public will rate your performance live so be sharp, don't miss and keep on kicking ass... or heads !
