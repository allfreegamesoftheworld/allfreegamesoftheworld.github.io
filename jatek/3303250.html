Minimális gépigény

Minimum:
  • OS: Windows 10 (64-bit)
  • Processor: Intel Core i5-7500 or better
  • Memory: 5 GB RAM
  • Graphics: NVIDIA GeForce GTX 970 or AMD RX 480 or better
  • DirectX: Version 11
  • Storage: 15 GB available space
  • Sound Card: DirectX 11 compatible
  • VR Support: Quest 2 and above. Any older discontinued VR headsets might have issues.
