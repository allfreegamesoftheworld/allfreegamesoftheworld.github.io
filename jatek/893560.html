Röviden a játékról

Spire of Sorcery is the upcoming open world survival RPG from Alexey Bokulev (creator of Eador. Genesis and Gremlins, Inc.). This free companion tool for the game allows you to generate and edit characters (biographies, portraits) from the world of Rund, and share them with the community.
