Röviden a játékról

Zombie Ants VR is a short (5-10 minute) experience that takes the player through the life cycle of Ophiocordyceps, a fungus that drives creatures (in this case ants) to change their behavior in order to propagate the fungus and ultimately kills them.

Minimális gépigény

Minimum:
  • Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system
  • OS: Windows 10
  • Processor: Intel Core i9
  • Memory: 64 GB RAM
  • Graphics: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3090
  • VR Support: Steam VR (verified Oculus Quest 2 and HTC Vive Pro)
  • Additional Notes: Student-driven project with variable performance. Minimum requirements represent the device most tested on which should maintain 60-120 FPS throughout the experience. Lower spec devices may still perform similarly.
