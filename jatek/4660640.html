Röviden a játékról

Blue Oath is a solo combat action-adventure game in which you play as a warrior swordfish who sets out on a journey to free a giant, cursed moray eel. Using powerful dash attacks, you can impale enemies and objects onto your beak, stack them, and throw them back as weapons or tools.
