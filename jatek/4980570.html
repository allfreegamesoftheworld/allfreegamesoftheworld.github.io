Röviden a játékról

Buzzness Tycoon is a cozy idle game where bees drift across a floating meadow, gathering pollen and carrying it home to make honey. Spend honey to upgrade faster bees, bigger grids, and labs, then relax and watch your little world hum along through a full day-night cycle.
