Röviden a játékról

Cartoony Cars 2 is a physics-based puzzle game about getting the car to the flag with limited resources. When you hit &quot;GO!&quot; the car drives by itself. Use sticks, boostpads, portals and the (not so) realistic physics engine to guide the duck to the flag!
