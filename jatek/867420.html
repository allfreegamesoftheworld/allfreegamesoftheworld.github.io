Röviden a játékról

Andor - Wrecking Dawn is a cross-platform(Pc &amp; Mobile) multi-player online card game (CCG). The game has five factions and about 500 magic cards to choose from, filled with infinite possibilities of deep strategy combinations. Players can build their decks with their own preferences to compete with others.
