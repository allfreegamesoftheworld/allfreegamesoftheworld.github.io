Röviden a játékról

Strategic Mind: Spirit of Liberty is a history-driven turn-based strategy set in WW2. Lead the scarce Finnish forces through the Winter War, Continuation War, and Lapland War. Save your country’s very existence from the Red Menace. Niet Molotoff!

Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Strategic Mind: Spirit of Liberty is a strategic simulation game achieving a realistic depiction of war from the command war room perspective. As such, it touches of themes of violence and death, but does not provide any graphic representation of gore and violence - just a symbolic representation of thereof.
