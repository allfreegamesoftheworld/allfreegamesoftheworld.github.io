Röviden a játékról

A fun 2D puzzle physics platformer where you manipulate the direction of gravity to complete your mission. You are Marvin, a cool stick-man who is abruptly disturbed by the Dream Wizard. Marvin is told he owes a &quot;dream debt&quot; to the Dream Wizard and will not be allowed to wake up until it's paid!
