Minimális gépigény

Minimum:
  • Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system
  • OS: Windows 10 64-bit
  • Processor: Intel i5-6600K, AMD 1600X or better
  • Memory: 8 GB RAM
  • Graphics: NVIDIA GTX 770; AMD Radeon 6950 HD; AMD Vega 8 IGP (Intel IGP not supported); 4GB VRAM or better
  • DirectX: Version 11
  • Storage: 1600 MB available space
  • Additional Notes: Minimum average sustained frame rate of 25-30fps or better achieved in 4-player split-screen on hardware combinations in this min-spec range. Your experience may vary based on a variety of factors. Broadband internet connection required for Remote Play Together multiplayer features.
