Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Keyboard Soldier contains minimal depictions of violence, focused on the combat against zombie enemies. Players will use firearms and explosives to eliminate these undead foes, which may result in mild blood effects and occasional explosions. The representation of violence is kept minimal and is stylized to fit the action-survival theme, with no excessive gore or graphic details. The game does not include any sexual content, drug or alcohol use, or depictions of self-harm.
