Adventure Craft is a procedurally generated open-world PvE survival crafting action RPG. Explore, gather resources, hunt monsters, craft weapons and armour, build a home, train companions, and survive a dangerous world that changes from day to night.

Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Adventure Craft contains stylized pixel-art violence and dark post-apocalyptic themes. Players can fight and kill hostile creatures, animals and human characters using melee weapons, firearms and other attacks. Combat can include red blood, bleeding effects, corpses and infected or monstrous enemies. The game also includes horror elements such as plague-infected creatures, demons and vampires. Violence is presented in a stylized pixel-art/comic aesthetic rather than realistically, and the game does not contain explicit sexual content or graphic gore.
