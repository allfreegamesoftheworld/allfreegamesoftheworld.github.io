Röviden a játékról

It's the late 1990s all over again and you find yourself the owner of Dave's Wrecking Yard. Earn cash by filling various parts orders by phone, internet, &amp; walk-in customers. New engine rebuilding shop will keep you busy, but enjoy working at your own pace while ordering pizzas and chugging sodas.
