Minimális gépigény

Minimum:
  • Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system
  • OS: Windows 10 64-bit
  • Processor: Intel Core i5-8400 / AMD Ryzen 5 2600 or better
  • Memory: 16 GB RAM
  • Graphics: NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 6 GB / AMD Radeon RX 580 8 GB (DirectX 12, Shader Model 6)
  • DirectX: Version 12
  • Storage: 10 GB available space
  • VR Support: PC VR over OpenXR, tested on Meta Quest 2 with Quest Link. VR Explore mode: flight and looking only; the climb, docking and the info panel are flat-screen. Tracked motion controllers or a gamepad. In VR, set the Windows audio output to the headset (Headphones, Oculus Virtual Audio Device) to hear the game there.
  • Additional Notes: Low to Medium settings at 1080p. Steam must be running for the leaderboard.
