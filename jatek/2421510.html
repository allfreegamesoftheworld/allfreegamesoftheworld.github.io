Röviden a játékról

Crescent Quest is a mostly medieval turn based open world RPG that is inspired by 2000s MMO / MMORPG designs while being a single player experience. Increase your skills, go on unique quests, grind for and collect rare items, join the world of Reltionus which is over ten years old!
