Röviden a játékról

Blind Bullet is a &quot;blind&quot; 2D action shooting game. The player cannot see but can perceive their surroundings through echolocation generated by footsteps. Shoot down unknown enemies with a shotgun, investigate the area with companions, and uncover the truth behind what has happened to them.
