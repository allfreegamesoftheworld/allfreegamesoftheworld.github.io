The game is a large-scale fantasy faction-warfare MMORPG. It contains frequent depictions of combat and fantasy violence, including the use of martial weapons, magical skills, and non-realistic visual effects for blood and character defeat during PvP and PvE encounters. Some female character designs or costumes may feature mildly suggestive or revealing clothing. The in-game text and story dialogue may occasionally include mild profanity or themes of war. The game also includes online interactive elements such as text chat and in-game purchases.
