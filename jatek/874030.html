Röviden a játékról

To the Core is a roguelite action-platforming speedrunner. Run, jump and dash your way through levels filled with obstacles and hazards. Fight through heaps of enemies to save your father and set record times.

Minimális gépigény

Minimum:
  • Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system
  • OS *: Windows 7 or higher
  • Processor: Quad-core 1.6GHz or higher (Intel Atom x7-series, or AMD equivalent). SSE2 support required.
  • Memory: 4 GB RAM
  • Graphics: NVIDIA GeForce GTX 8xx or higher (or AMD equivalent). Lower-end GPUs or onboard GPUs may work, but support cannot be guaranteed. Screen resolution of 1280x720 or higher required.
  • DirectX: Version 11
  • Network: Broadband Internet connection
  • Storage: 2 GB available space
