Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Violence — zombies are shot and killed using firearms. Hit effects include blood splatter at point of impact. Weapons — realistic weapon handling including recoil, reload mechanics, and multiple firearm types (pistol, rifle, SMG, sniper, shotgun). Gore — blood effects spawn on zombie hits with visual damage numbers. Some of the zombies have no clothes but they have no sex organs.
