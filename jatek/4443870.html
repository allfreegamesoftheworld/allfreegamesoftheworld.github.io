Röviden a játékról

My Last War is an experimental World War I kinetic novel about fighting and trying to find meaning in a fallen world. You assume the role of Maria Strauss, a 19-year-old sergeant in the German army who, after experiencing a revealing dream, decides she will do whatever it takes to stop the war.
