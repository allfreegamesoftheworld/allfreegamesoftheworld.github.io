Röviden a játékról

Arcane Archer is a 3D platforming FPS speedrun game where you must use explosive arrows to blast yourself across gaps and up walls to reach the goal! Use the power of your explosive arrows to parkour your way through an assortment of stages, and shoot for the top of the leaderboard!
