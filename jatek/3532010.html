Röviden a játékról

Art House is a 2D puzzle platformer in which you play as a woman who gets trapped with her children inside the paintings of her recently deceased mother. Navigate through painting rips and use a magical paintbrush to escape and come to terms with your grief.

Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Art House contains narrative themes involving death, memory loss, and the emotional impact of grief. Sensitive players should be aware that the game deals with cognitive deterioration and loss, though all story moments are presented thoughtfully and can be skipped.
