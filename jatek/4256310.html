S.P.E.C.T.O.R 01 is a Third-Person SCI-FI action shooter. You are a soldier in the distant future on a solo rescue mission codenamed S.P.E.C.T.O.R 01. Aliens have taken over certain areas around earth and have taken one of your fellow soldiers hostage. Your task is to bring them to safety.
