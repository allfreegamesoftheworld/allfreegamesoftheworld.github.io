Röviden a játékról

A surreal group of generic ATMs acts like bumper cars in an arena, roaming around set on a desolate square quay in an infinite ocean. As a first-person player, you ram into other bot ATMs before they hit you. This is an indie art video game by Studio Alona Rodeh, Berlin, Germany.
