Röviden a játékról

Yugi-inspired card game -roguelike with an extra deck. Enemies use the same cards / rules as you. Reduce opponent's life from 20 to 0; by playing cards and attacking. Pick cards of 3-choices after each win. Currently 120 unique cards, 5 floors, and the final boss.
