Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Bad Mineral contains somewhat cartoonic violence and gore, with large drops of blood that fall from characters, exposed zombie guts, decapitation and flying skull and brain fragments. The game also has a theme of hard drug use, where players take an unspecified substance for the purpose of combat. Drug use is not shown, but they have bloody noses, and there is a snorting sound, followed by characters saying something like "Oh, yeah." Gibs, blood and decapitation can be turned off in settings.
