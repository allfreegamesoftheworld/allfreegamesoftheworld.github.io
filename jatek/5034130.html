Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Crystaria Reborn contains fantasy combat violence. Players fight monsters, elemental creatures and bosses using swords, magic and other abilities. Combat is shown in a stylised pixel-art style, with damage numbers and spell effects. There is no realistic gore, and defeated enemies simply fade away. Some abilities and class themes refer to blood magic and life-draining, shown as stylised red visual effects. The game contains no sexual content or nudity, and does not depict drug or alcohol abuse, self-harm or sexual violence. This is an online game with player chat. Other players' messages are not moderated in advance. Players can block anyone using the in-game Blacklist.
