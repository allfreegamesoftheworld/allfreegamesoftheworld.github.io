Röviden a játékról

In Catacombs, you control Tabby, a street cat on his way to visit a friend in the afterlife. Unfortunately, Osiris himself forbade your entry and ordered his minions to stop you. Venture deep into the catacombs, pouncing to assume control over his minions, all to foil the will of the gods!
