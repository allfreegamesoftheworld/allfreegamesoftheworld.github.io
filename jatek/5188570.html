A Rover's Journey is a 3D, narrative, exploration game, set in space! Play as Roo-ver, and help your astronaut companion discover all the lunar landscape has to offer. Traverse difficult terrain, avoid crashing meteors, and uncover tech from previous expeditions. Can the moon sustain human life?
