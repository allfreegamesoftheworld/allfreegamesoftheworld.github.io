Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

This game features intense violence, including graphic depictions of enemies exploding with blood and gore, leaving visceral stains on the environment. The sound design emphasizes the brutal nature of combat, with effects that suggest slicing through flesh and violent dismemberment. Some enemy character designs include revealing outfits, with partial nudity. This content is intended for mature audiences and may not be suitable for all players.
