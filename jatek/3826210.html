Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

This game offers a virtual pub management experience and includes: - Gameplay mechanics involving the sale of alcohol. - Scenes that feature alcohol as part of the simulation elements, without promoting its consumption. The game is designed as an entertaining management simulation and does not intend to encourage real-life alcohol use. Players are encouraged to make responsible decisions as a pub owner.
