Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Elderwood features stylized fantasy violence. Players fight monsters and other players using melee weapons, bows, and magic. Characters can be defeated in combat, and deaths in designated PvP areas can result in the loss of carried items. Combat uses low-poly graphics without graphic gore or dismemberment. Online interactions include player-written chat and character names.
