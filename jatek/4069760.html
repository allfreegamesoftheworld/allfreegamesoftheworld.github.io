Röviden a játékról

VR AIM is a VR-first aim training platform built for players who take VR shooters seriously. Unlike traditional or flat-screen-inspired aim trainers, VR AIM focuses on real techniques, real recoil, and real hardware setups. The skills you train actually transfer into gameplay.

Minimális gépigény

Minimum:
  • OS: Windows 10 (64-bit)
  • Processor: Intel i5-4590 / AMD Ryzen 5 1500X or better
  • Memory: 8 GB RAM
  • Graphics: NVIDIA GTX 1060 / AMD Radeon RX 480 or better
  • DirectX: Version 11
  • Network: Broadband Internet connection
  • Storage: 500 MB available space
  • Sound Card: Standard onboard audio
  • VR Support: OpenXR, SteamVR, Oculus Rift, Meta Quest (via Link), HTC Vive, Valve Index, Windows Mixed Reality
  • Additional Notes: Requires VR headset and motion controllers
