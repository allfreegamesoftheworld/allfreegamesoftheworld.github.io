Röviden a játékról

Draw toward 20. Turn the tables with + cards. TWENTY+ is a head-to-head card game with a shared deck numbered 1 to 11. Draw, stand, or change the target itself. Read your opponent's hidden card and time your + cards to swing the match. Play online or against the CPU.
