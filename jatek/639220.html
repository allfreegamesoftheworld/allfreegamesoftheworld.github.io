Röviden a játékról

Welcome to a fresh multiplayer, VR, racing, freestyle and Soccer experience. Each VR tracked controller is a mono-wheel motorized rocket. You have freedom to race and perform freestyle jumps and maneuvers in VR using the physics principle of sports like snowboard, skate and motocross.
