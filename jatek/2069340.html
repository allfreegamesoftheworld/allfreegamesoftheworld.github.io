Röviden a játékról

Varial is a single player skateboarding-action game where you play as the titular character fighting a giant robot called the &quot;Y.2.K.A.I.J.U.&quot;. The game takes place in a Y2K apocalypse, where all the technology has revolted against humanity and wiped out most of the population.
