Röviden a játékról

Swoop is a fast-paced shedding card game. Play cards equal to or lower than the center, or pick up the pile! Take risks with mystery cards, use Jokers or 10's to swoop the board, and host multiplayer lobbies with Steam friends or challenge advanced AI. Can you empty your hand first?
