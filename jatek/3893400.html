Röviden a játékról

This is a frame-by-frame platformer. In an abstract, childlike world, command the CatFather and make frame-level adjustments to ensure he safely reaches the next paper tube and returns to his cat tree. Help the CatFather ascend to the peak of Huff-and-Puff Summit!
