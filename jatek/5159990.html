Röviden a játékról

Arduz is a free-to-play 2D MMORPG set in a medieval fantasy world. Choose your class and race, master magic, team up with friends and fight epic PvP battles in quick online matches. A classic old-school MMO: easy to learn, light on resources and quick to play.
