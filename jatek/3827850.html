Röviden a játékról

StacKit is a turn-based puzzle of pure strategy. Load your gun in a precise order before you fight—this is your 'Stack'. Once in combat, your firing sequence is locked. Use Fire to strip armor, then hit with Corrosion to weaken foes and boost all other damage.
