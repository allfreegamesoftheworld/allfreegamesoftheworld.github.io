Röviden a játékról

HARDPOINT is an intense FPS tower defense hybrid. You are mining a rare mineral on the desert planet Veoda, and the local robotic defense network wants you gone. Fight on the ground with your own weapons, build and configure automated turrets, upgrade your stats, and keep your generators alive.
