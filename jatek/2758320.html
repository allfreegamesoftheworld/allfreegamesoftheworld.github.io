Röviden a játékról

In this twist on minesweeper, find all treasures while avoiding mines to clear stages. Use items, deduce from nearby counts, or guess to identify tiles. Features a large-scale mode with multiple stages leading to a final treasure, and a small-scale mode focused on deducing a single stage.
