Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Blood & Lineage features frequent combat and gameplay elements that may not be suitable for all audiences. Violence: Players engage in melee and/or ranged combat against various enemies. This includes depictions of blood-splatter effects upon impact and the death of characters during gameplay. Gambling Elements: The game includes a "Forge" system where players use in-game resources to attempt to upgrade or acquire items. This mechanic involves elements of chance and risk/reward similar to gambling, though it uses strictly in-game currency/items and does not involve real-world money.
