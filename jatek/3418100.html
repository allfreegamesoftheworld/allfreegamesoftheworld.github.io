Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Faithfall features mild depictions of blood. The game's narrative revolves around a fictional religious organization that draws inspiration from structures and traditions reminiscent of the Catholic Church. However, it is not intended as a direct reference to or representation of any real-world institution.
