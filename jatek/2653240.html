Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Our game includes a tournament mode with duels, where hitting opponents results in a minimal blood effect. These are non-lethal, friendly matches. The only characters that can be killed are fictional rats. Some NPCs in the game may be rude and rarely use slurs.
