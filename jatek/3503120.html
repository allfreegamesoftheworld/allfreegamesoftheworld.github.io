Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

This game is an educational game designed to help players learn how to support children with disabilities. The game includes the following scenes: •A scene where a child experiences a seizure (depicted for educational purposes to provide medical knowledge). •Illustrations of medical care, such as gastrostomy (G-tube feeding) and catheterization (used as educational material for learning proper medical support). These elements are intended to help players gain the necessary knowledge to support children with disabilities, particularly those interested in the medical and welfare fields. The game does not contain excessive violence or sexual content.
