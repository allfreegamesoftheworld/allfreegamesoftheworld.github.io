Röviden a játékról

Voipas is a virtual assistant and includes a chat feature. You can add only 7 friends. There is also a mobile version. The NPC can give you an advice when you feel down, cheer you, give you a virtual kiss or remind you to study for exams. You can own a dog and pet him whenever you want.
