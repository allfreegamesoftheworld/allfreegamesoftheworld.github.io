Röviden a játékról

Snake, except you never die to a missed keypress. Your line steers itself around walls and bodies, so every death is a rival outplaying you. Plan routes, cut off space, force rivals into dead ends, and turn the match with lasers, warps and bombs. Up to 4 players, online (rollback netcode) or local.
