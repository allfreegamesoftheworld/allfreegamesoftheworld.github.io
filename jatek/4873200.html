Röviden a játékról

MR TWIDDLE'S WEE DIDDY FARM DELUXE is a resource management roguelike of agricultural theming. Use farming to convert plants into materials for selling and potion brewing. Use a variety of crops and items to punch your way through hordes of chickens.
