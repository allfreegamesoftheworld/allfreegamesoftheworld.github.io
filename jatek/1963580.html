Röviden a játékról

The Lizartian is a top-down action-adventure game. We will embody a lizard tamer, a Lizartian, who will have to travel the vast lands of Dekoto to enact his revenge. Along the way, he'll face dangers like savage bandits, rugged terrain, and the legendary sandstorm.
