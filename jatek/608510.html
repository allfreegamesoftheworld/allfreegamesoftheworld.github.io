Röviden a játékról

It's the 1990s. You’re alone at home, and you heard about the &quot;adult&quot; internet at school. You Must be 18 or Older to Enter blurs several genres together to create an anxious horror experience, one that hits a little too close. How deep will you dive before your parents come home? Do you even like this stuff?
