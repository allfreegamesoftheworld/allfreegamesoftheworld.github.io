Röviden a játékról

Basically, the purpose of this game is to pass a level, and listen to the music. Pass a level, and try a harder one. Just use your mouse to choose, and use your spacebar to play, EASY!!! Everyone can play the music. Now, there are 11 different songs in the game, and there will be more. You can also upload your own song in the game.
