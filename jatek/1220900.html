Röviden a játékról

Astonia Remastered is the 'remastered' version of Astonia 3. The game features oldschool RPG mechanics such as highly customizable characters, a high variety of spells, skills and professions, complex quests in a rich high fantasy/steam-punk setting, featuring an intricate PK and PVP system.
