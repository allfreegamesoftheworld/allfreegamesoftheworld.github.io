Röviden a játékról

Chicken Chess Match is a unique addition to the Match-3 genre. Pieces move like they do in Chess. Catch chicken's before they cross the board by matching pieces they are standing on. Earn chips and use them to purchase powerups. Earn combo points by catching 2 birds in one match!
