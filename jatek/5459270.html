Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Revenge on the 13th Floor features frequent stylized, cartoon-like violence depicted entirely through ASCII graphics and a retro 16-color palette. Players use firearms and a knife to fight human enemies. Combat includes shooting, melee attacks, exaggerated ASCII blood splatters, persistent bloodstains, and fallen bodies. The game also contains frequent strong language and profanity. Despite its violent themes, all visuals are highly abstract and deliberately unrealistic, inspired by classic DOS games and '80s arcade aesthetics. The game contains no realistic gore, nudity, or sexual content.
