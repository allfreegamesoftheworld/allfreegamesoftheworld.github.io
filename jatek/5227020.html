Röviden a játékról

Survive five increasingly dangerous nights in a psychological horror game where the cameras are your eyes and the flashlight is your defense. Monitor the entity, prevent it from attacking, and repair sabotaged systems in order to survive a terrible end because the nights won't be forgiving.
