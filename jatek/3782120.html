Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

This game contains elements of psychological horror that may be disturbing to some players. Aspects of this game's story touch upon themes like the following. - Characters having a mental breakdown, traumatized, mentally tortured. - The story content depicts themes such as childhood trauma, amnesia, suicide, depression, and parental violence. - Part of the story's content covers the theme of a mass disastrous incident, post-apocalyptic world, death, grief, mourning, loss, and loneliness. - The game contains themes related to the concept of 4th wall breaking.
