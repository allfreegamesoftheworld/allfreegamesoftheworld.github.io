Röviden a játékról

Chicken Fren is a desktop pet that struts across your screen while you work or play. Every mouse click and keypress is a chance to spawn an egg: crack the egg to reveal hats and cosmetics ranging from common to legendary. Collect items, equip them, or trade them on the Steam Marketplace!
