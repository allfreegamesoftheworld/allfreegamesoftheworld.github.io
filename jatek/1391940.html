Röviden a játékról

DogFight is a &quot;Windows mixed reality&quot;- headset flight combat experience game. The focus of the game is to give a virtual reality cockpit experience of a world war 2 pilot. The game can be played using basic keyboard controls without having to deal with complex flight simulation controls.
