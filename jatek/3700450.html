Röviden a játékról

Lootimals is a chill idle pet collection game where you hatch surprise eggs every 30 minutes. Discover uniquely named creatures across rarity tiers, unlock backgrounds and egg skins, complete collections, and trade in extras for missing pets. No combat, no pressure — just pure collecting bliss.
