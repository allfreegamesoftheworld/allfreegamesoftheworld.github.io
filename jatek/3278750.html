Röviden a játékról

From an agent embedded in a group of Russian mafia, serving the drug cartels, the FDCN of Russia receives information about the supply of heroin by sea. The agent gives the approximate coordinates of the place of the goods, which should take place near an labor camp on the Pacific coast of Russia.
