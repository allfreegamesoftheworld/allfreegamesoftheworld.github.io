Röviden a játékról

Wield your telekinetic voice in Resonance, a short twin-stick top-down action game. Sweep away enemies, raise and hurl objects, use them as a shield, and escape the dark facility trying to contain your powers. Nominated for Best Student Game at the Dutch Game Awards 2025.
