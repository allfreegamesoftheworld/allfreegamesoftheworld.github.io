Röviden a játékról

The Hellchemist is an intense pve arena shooter where you will fight waves of monsters while random events will alter the way you have to play the game and makes every run unique. The game is heavily inspired by Doom and Quake. Bunny hops, shooting and a whole lot of explosions!
