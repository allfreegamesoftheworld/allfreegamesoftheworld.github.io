Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

This cartoony game is based on a Bible passage telling you to kill everything that breathes in the promised land. You do this by chopping them up with your magic laser sword causing blood to spray everywhere. There are also sexual worship assistants who stand around in their underwear. The Bible passage says that God hates the way the people worship their gods.
