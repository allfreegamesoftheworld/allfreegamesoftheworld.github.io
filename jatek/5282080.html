Röviden a játékról

Dungeon Raiders is a dungeon exploration game. Illuminate the dark with voice lights, avoid monsters, collect relics, and escape safely. Sell relics to gear up and meet the company quota every 3 days. One more look, or head back now? Challenge yourself against escalating quotas in limited time.
