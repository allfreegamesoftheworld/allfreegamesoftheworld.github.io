Röviden a játékról

This is a derivative work of the 1990s anime game [Serial Experiments Lain]. It was created using RPG Maker MZ and based on the official guidelines &quot;Lain Time to Live&quot;.The game is about exploring dungeons and reading the scenario. I think you can play through the entire content in about 2-3 hours.
