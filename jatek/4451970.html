Röviden a játékról

An arcade-style tactics/strategy mini-game set in space. Players pilot a spaceship or a fleet of spaceships and navigate them past a blockade of guard ships so they can arrive safely at their home planet. Players can complete the game's 19 levels in about 1 hour.
