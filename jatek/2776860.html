Röviden a játékról

&quot;Let's Patiti!&quot; is a multiplayer party game that supports 2-4 players and can be played online or locally. In the game, you take on the role of an adorable little cat, engaging in a game of chase and cunning competition with your &quot;Meow Meow&quot; friends or AI in the &quot;Meow Meow Forest&quot;.
