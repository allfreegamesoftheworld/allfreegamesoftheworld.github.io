Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

This game contains a chase and slash game mode that uses sharp or blunt weapons, the screen will have a blood splash effect on a successful attack and during the execution event. The setting option "Bloody Mode" is available in the game. Turning off the "Bloody Mode" can change the blood color to green and has a mosaic occlusion when executed.
