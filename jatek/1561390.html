Röviden a játékról

StarStruck is a fast-paced side-scrolling platformer with a uniquely bizarre aesthetic. The game features 12 weird worlds, each with a 36 second timer to complete, but don’t expect a quick and easy experience. Making it through the game will require master-level platformer play.
