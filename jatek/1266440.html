Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

This game depicts cartoonish mutilation of the player in order to gain abilities to use to traverse the game. You can expect blood splatters and the depictions of various organs being used outside of the body. You will use these abilities, along with other attacks to fight off monsters that you encounter through the game.
