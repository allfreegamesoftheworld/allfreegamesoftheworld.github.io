Röviden a játékról

Naib's Adventure is a fan-made RPG inspired by the world of Identity V. In this game, you will take on the role of the battle-hardened mercenary, Naib Subedar, who receives a mysterious commission from the priestess Fiona to investigate a cursed and remote village.
