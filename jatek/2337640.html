Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Pinball FX Midnight lets the player explore fan-favorite horror and thriller franchises in pinball form. The themes of the tables are action-packed, and while playing, you’ll encounter gory, scary, or spooky scenes. While the characters perform violent acts, the game of pinball is still a skill-based game with an established ruleset, and as such the scenes mentioned are short and stay within the clearly defined space of a pinball machine. The game features no sexual content.
