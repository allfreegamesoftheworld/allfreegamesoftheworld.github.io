Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

This game includes scenes of women in hot springs or baths, where they are unclothed. However, any sensitive areas are hidden underwater, and there is no explicit sexual content or excessive exposure. It also features depictions of alcohol consumption, including portrayals of alcohol dependence, though these are mainly presented in a humorous manner.
