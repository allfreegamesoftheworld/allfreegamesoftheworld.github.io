Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Playworlds contains frequent violence and gore, including realistic blood, killing, bodily injury, corpses, and potentially extreme depictions such as mutilation, torture, and detailed deaths. It also contains psychological horror, mild profanity, some nudity or sexual content, depictions of sexual violence or abuse, and references to alcohol, tobacco, misuse of medical drugs, and illegal drug use. Because some dialogue and story content is generated live by AI, the exact presentation can vary between sessions.
