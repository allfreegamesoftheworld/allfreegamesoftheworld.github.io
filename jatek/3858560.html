Röviden a játékról

TunTunWorld is a 3D action game with a third-person perspective. Players control a character to explore the map, using three main actions: poke, grab, and jump. By investigating various areas, they can uncover and solve mysteries. The ultimate goal is to solve every puzzle and clear the game.
