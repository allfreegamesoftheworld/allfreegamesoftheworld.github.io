Röviden a játékról

Poop Rocket is a casual sci-fi 2D shooter game. Bomb your way through debris and aliens before the poop power meter runs out.  Avoid the debris and aliens, or you'll take damage and die.  The game is controlled with your keyboard.   Use the arrows to navigate and the space bar to drop bombs.
