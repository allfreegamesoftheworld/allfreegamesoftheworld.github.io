Röviden a játékról

Meet a new friend in the dreamlike City of Rivers. One Night in Kawami is a short visual novel about meeting new friends in an unknown city and possibly reshaping reality as you thought you knew it.

Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

During the last stretch of the game, you will make a choice with deadly implications to a faraway location of your choosing. Most of the aftermath of your interaction will be delivered by text alone.
