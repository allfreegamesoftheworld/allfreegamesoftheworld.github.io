Journey back to the nostalgic time of the 1980’s and then step into the future of video games. Based on the &quot;futuristic&quot; arcade games of the 80’s and 90’s, Trigger Finger is a virtual reality game meant to test your reflexes while giving you a sense for a time that some of us weren’t alive for.
