Röviden a játékról

Mesozoic Dawn is a multiplayer online dinosaur survival game. Starting as a vulnerable juvenile dinosaur, you must navigate the perils of the prehistoric wilderness, utilizing the core &quot;Growth + Nesting = Evolution&quot; system to achieve leapfrog species iteration and evolution.
