Röviden a játékról

Space Aces is a video game made for the spring 2022 offering of CAP 4053 at UCF. The player controls a space fighter capable of movement in 3 directions and rotation in 3 directions. The game supports any controller, keyboard, and the Thrustmaster 1600M flight stick.

Minimális gépigény

Minimum:
  • Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system
  • OS: 20H2
  • Processor: i5-8400
  • Memory: 16 GB RAM
  • Graphics: 1070TI
  • Storage: 2 GB available space
  • Sound Card: Integrated
  • Additional Notes: These are my specs, but it will probably run on lesser equipment. One time, Austin ran the game on a laptop without a dedicated graphics card, and another time I downloaded the itch distribution and was able to run it on a work computer that is very old and has no dedicated graphics card. I don't have the resources to find out more.
