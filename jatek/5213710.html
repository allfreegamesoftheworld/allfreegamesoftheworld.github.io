Röviden a játékról

Homeward on the Wind is a story-driven life simulation set in the town of Clearbrook in 1992. Return to the past as Shen Mingzhu, revive her mother’s old general store, manage stock and upgrades, explore the town, meet its residents, and shape relationships through your choices.
