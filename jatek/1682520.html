Röviden a játékról

A virtual gallery filled with art, inspired by games published by Raw Fury. Games are art. In our mind this is a fact. Games, along with movies, books, music, artwork… they are an artform. They can touch people. To change minds and hearts. It´s our charge to not only view them so but treat them so.
