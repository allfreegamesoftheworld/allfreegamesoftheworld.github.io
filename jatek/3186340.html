Röviden a játékról

In STAaRS, you play as a volunteer investigating unknown activities in a simulated environment. During a 24-hour test, events appear randomly, and you enter the virtual house via a portal to investigate. Observe, categorize, and report events based on Space, Time, Anomalies, and Reality.
