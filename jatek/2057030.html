Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

In No More Noise, players face intense, suspense-filled encounters in a post-apocalyptic setting. The game features depictions of violence, as the player must fight against undead enemies with a variety of weapons, including firearms and melee options. Combat involves realistic impacts, injury effects, and occasional blood spatters. Additionally, the game’s core sound mechanic increases the danger, creating heightened tension and fear that might be intense for some players.
