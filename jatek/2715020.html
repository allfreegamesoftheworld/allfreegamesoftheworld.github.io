Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

This game revolves around vampires in a horror setting and will contain many depictions or references to many amoral, sensitive, disturbing, and monstrous things, such as drinking blood, murder, manipulation, exploitation, suicide, abuse, and religion. This is not an excuse to be a monster yourself. Due to the use of historical settings, some stories may contain references to old fashioned attitudes that may not match modern sensibilities. This game is not suitable for those who are easily offended or upset by such content.
