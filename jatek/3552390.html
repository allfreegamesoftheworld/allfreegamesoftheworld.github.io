Röviden a játékról

Kiwi's Adventure is a fangame made for the Avalon Vtuber group. Play as Kiwi, Narin's adorable mascot, and go on a quest to find her after she has gone missing! Travel through the realm of each Avalon member, gaining gifts of power from each of them to help you reach Narin!
