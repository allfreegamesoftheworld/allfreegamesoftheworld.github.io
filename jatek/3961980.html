Röviden a játékról

Critters’ Breakout is a chaotic 3D chase adventure where you can play as the clever Critters escaping to freedom or as the fierce Kollectors trying to stop them. Scavenge items, outsmart enemies, and fight to survive. It is a Pinoy-made game that directly funds animal shelters when you play!
