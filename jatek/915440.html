Röviden a játékról

Fireflies is a First Person Psychological Adventure. The core mechanics revolves around exploration, survival from the enemy and a bit puzzle solving elements added to it. You open drawers, doors, inspect objects, unravel the mysteries of the unknown, meet interesting characters.
