Röviden a játékról

Jump, dash and slide through a futuristic garden in Palette Cleanser, cute 3D platformer focused on smooth movement and an optimistic vibe! Control a tiny robot trying to restore the color palette of its garden. Capture the alien invaders to activate paint sprinklers and make the garden alive again.
