Röviden a játékról

VR Hybrid War 2117 is an intense, action packed space shooting game. It has laser pistols, metallic enemy spiders, explosions, probes flying around trying to seek and destroy you. The mission is to destroy the enemy probe factory, liquid oxygen tank, space ship and a radar.
