Röviden a játékról

Fighters Guild is a fantasy roguelite with tactical 1v1 combat and base building. Go on procedurally generated raids, develop your guild, and craft builds from abilities, gear, and artifacts. The guild grows and evolves. Mercenaries are expendable.

Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

The game contains fantasy combat with melee attacks and abilities including burning, freezing, poisoning, and bleeding. All effects are presented as stylized VFX animations with no realistic blood or gore. Players can bet in a tavern using in-game gold earned through gameplay — no real money involved. No sexual content or graphic violence.
