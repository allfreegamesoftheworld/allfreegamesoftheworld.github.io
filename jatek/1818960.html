Röviden a játékról

ModBots is a third-person arena rogue-like in which players play as robots fighting through randomized arena layouts against hordes of grunt robots and larger gladiators. As the players advance, they gain upgrades and weapons to enhance their abilities to fight and survive in the everchanging arena!
