Röviden a játékról

Kritzelkrieg is Age of War on squared notebook paper – except you draw your whole army yourself. Ink is power: more pixels = more HP and damage, but pricier. Build your base floor by floor, unlock new classes and send your doodle army marching. Versus a bot or online 1v1.
