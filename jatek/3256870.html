Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

The game addresses contemporary themes that encourage reflection, exploring philosophical questions regarding the nature of life and the human and in general sentient life condition. It also delves into thoughts about mortality and related topics. For these reasons, I believe the content is not suitable for a child audience as it might feel too complex to be really understood.
