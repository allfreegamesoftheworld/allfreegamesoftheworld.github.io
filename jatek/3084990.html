Röviden a játékról

This game is a parody created under the influence of the hype of crypto airdrops in clickers. Click on a coin to mine coins, sell coins for other coins, buy video cards to automate mining. Buy chests for mined coins to try to get new types of coins or get it on market or by exchanging with friends.
