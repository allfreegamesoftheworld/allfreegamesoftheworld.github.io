Röviden a játékról

Explore the endlessly generated dungeon as you kill monsters, level up, and collect loot in this first-person dungeon crawler. Magic items improve your character's stats so that it's easier to use better equipment and learn better spells. You choose how your character progresses.

Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Goblins, skeletons, ghosts, and other monsters carry weapons to attack the player with. There is a blood spatter effect on the screen when the player attacks with their weapon. Weapons can drop, be found in chests, or be purchased in town.
