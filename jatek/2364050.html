PUNCHERMAN!: First Day is a first-person comedy physics dad simulator where you play as a retired FPS hero/generally manly man trying to become the best dad ever. One problem: the only way he can interact with the world is with an incredible punch. Use your fists of fury to prove your heart of gold!
