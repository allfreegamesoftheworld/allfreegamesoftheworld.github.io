Röviden a játékról

Roll up a fighter, a wizard or an unlicensed bard. This free idle RPG fits three adventures in a Windows 98-style character sheet. Your adventurer fights and sells the loot while you're away. You pick their talents and decide whether to trust the directions in a letter from the dungeon.
