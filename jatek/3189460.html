Röviden a játékról

9 Years of Dreaming is an action-adventure game where you explore the subconscious of Jacob, who has been in a coma for nine years after a car accident. Trapped in a surreal dreamscape, Jacob meets Lumby, a mysterious guide who helps him on his journey to wake up.
