Röviden a játékról

Idle pinball meets pachinko in Flux Fall. Drop balls through a glowing peg cascade, use your flippers to keep them in play, and hit ramps to build bigger chains and more coins. Level up every part of the board, upgrade your automation, and keep earning while you're away.
