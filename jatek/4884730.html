Röviden a játékról

Avalon Clash is an 8-player online turn-based card battler fusing hidden identities with tactical card combat. Pick a legendary hero, draw a secret role, and duel with strikes, spells, and legendary weapons across a 111-card deck. Outplay rivals and smart AI in ranked and arena matches.
