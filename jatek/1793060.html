Röviden a játékról

PlanetDrop - A Tiny Space Adventure is a computer game where you command a spaceship in an orbit of a planet. Your primary task is to capture &quot;drop zones&quot; with your shuttles and commandos. You rely on stealth, agility and speed, rather than on firepower.
