Inspired by a dieselpunk aesthetic, navigate a winding factory space filled with environmental hazards and hostile robot workers with the goal of survival. Rise from the ashes! Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

The story of Turmoil depicts a man with a mechanical right arm. This arm gets caught in a conveyor belt during the opening scene of the game, leading it to be crushed between two metal objects. The gore is minimal as it is shown 2D stylized cutscene. The main character runs on oil rather than blood, so he leaks oil when his arm is crushed. This oil is the player's health and drains consistently throughout the game. The player's only survival option is to fight back against the factory robots that turned on him. The player must kill hostile robots to gain an oil canister pickup, which will provide a health boost
