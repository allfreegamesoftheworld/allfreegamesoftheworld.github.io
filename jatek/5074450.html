Röviden a játékról

Wanxiang Corridor: Elements is a 2D roguelike action shooter. Wield a bow through procedurally generated corridors, switching between Water and Ice to trigger Freeze and stun your foes. Clear rooms, pick randomized buffs, forge your build across five stages. Pixel art, a fresh run every time.
