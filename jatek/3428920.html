Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

In two of the minigames ( not "BOAT RACER") you fight Nazi's. In "Knazi Knockout" you beat them up MMA style, in "Nazi's in the Mist" you shoot them in an arena style FPS. The "death" and " gore" is minimal and more toward the cartoonish style than realistic. Images of women in Bikinis also on screen and in "Knazi Knockout" you can select wardrobe for your female fighter that can include bikini's or sexy outfits
