Röviden a játékról

Goofballs is a goofy physics-based soccer game made by students at SMU Guildhall. Players roll, dash, and ground pound their way to victory. With a variety of ball types, playing surfaces, and arenas there are hundreds of combinations to play! Proceeds go to support student development.
