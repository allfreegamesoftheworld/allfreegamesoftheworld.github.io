Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

VampOS is a vampire management game featuring recurring mature themes. Gameplay contains text- and UI-based references to killing and abducting humans, human trafficking, hypnotizing people into thralls, harvesting and consuming blood, recovering and processing dead bodies, and conducting organized crime. The game also contains prominent stylized body-horror imagery featuring blood, skulls, flesh-like structures, and occult themes. Violence is generally represented through menus, resource counters, and written operation descriptions rather than animated combat.
