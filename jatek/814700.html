Röviden a játékról

The Larkin Building build by Frank Lloyd Wright in 1904 is one of the most famous office building off all time. It has been demolished in 1954 and there where no color picture of the building till now. In this VR experience you can walk through the building and explore the spaces, furniture and atmosphere.
