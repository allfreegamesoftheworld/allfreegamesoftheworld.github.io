Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

The game contains non-graphic depictions of suicide and self-harm themes. In certain story scenes, characters imply through dialogue that they intend to take their own lives, followed by sound effects indicating that they collapse. The acts themselves are not shown visually. The game also contains fantasy violence and character deaths presented through pixel-art combat and dialogue. There are no graphic depictions of suicide, self-harm, extreme gore, sexual acts, sexual violence, or nudity.
