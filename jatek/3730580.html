Röviden a játékról

Enter the 3D sci-fi world of SolCycle! Play as a robot cat archaeologist, explore a post-human community rebuilt by plant-fueled robots, and barter with the locals in card-based gameplay! Learn to navigate the citizens’ requests while under the spell of a time-loop. Don’t forget to watch the clock!
