Röviden a játékról

Guardians of the Marble Man is a historical adventure set in 1864 Columbia, South Carolina, recreated through years of research. As a Time Gamer, you search for the lost Roman statue of John C. Calhoun, uncovering secrets tied to secession, war, and hidden history beneath the Old State House.
