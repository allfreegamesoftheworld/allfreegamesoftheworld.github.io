Röviden a játékról

Daycare Descent is a co-op shooter inspired by Left 4 Dead. Take control of our four trepid adventurers as they battle toys throughout the daycare in search of snacks. Tighten your diapers, grab your pacifiers, and fill up your water guns. Playtime's just begun!
