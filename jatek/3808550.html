Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Depending on the player's chosen scenario, players may engage in combat with other characters or creatures, resuilting in text depictions of violence and gore. Players guide the actions of the characters, meaning characters may use drugs or alcohol based on player action or setting. Players may also encounter non-explicit sexual content such as sexual langage or innuendos. Google's Gemini AI prevents explicit sexual content, but upon player bruteforcing actions may lead to 'Fade to black'-esque text depictions. The Image generation does not produce any explicit sexual content and prevents most violent/gory content, usually amounting to not more than depictions of blood.
