Röviden a játékról

Yurei Hunt is a multiplayer hide &amp; seek prop hunt game where deception is key. Play as a Yurei, possessing objects &amp; using abilities, or as a Hunter, relying on instincts &amp; proximity voice chat to track them. With a world full of tricks, surprises &amp; eerie tension, every match is a battle of wits.
