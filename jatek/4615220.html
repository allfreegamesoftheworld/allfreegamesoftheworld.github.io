EXPLODES is a fast-paced 2D stickman fighting game. Choose from 15 explosive weapons and battle across 10 unique arenas. Play solo against AI bots, fight friends locally on one keyboard, or take on players online. Level up, unlock gear, and dominate. Completely free to play with no paywalls.
