Minimális gépigény

Minimum:
  • OS: Windows 10 64-bit
  • Processor: Intel Core i3-6100 / AMD FX-6300
  • Memory: 4 GB RAM
  • Graphics: DirectX 11 compatible GPU, equivalent to Intel HD 530 / AMD Radeon R7 250
  • DirectX: Version 11
  • Network: Broadband Internet connection
  • Storage: 3 MB available space
  • Sound Card: DirectX-compatible sound card
  • VR Support: None
  • Additional Notes: Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system. Internet connection required to participate in the live global counter and community click submissions. 1280x720 supported. SSD recommended. Best experience at 1920x1080 with a stable internet connection.
