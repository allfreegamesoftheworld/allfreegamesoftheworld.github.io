Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

This game features frequent depictions of combat between robotic characters in a MOBA-style format, involving realistic and intense violence. Players will encounter scenes of destruction, explosions, and mechanical dismemberment, as well as sound effects and visuals designed to enhance the impact of battles. The game does not include depictions of sexual acts, nudity, or human violence, but its intense combat scenarios may not be suitable for all audiences. There are no references to drug or alcohol abuse, self-harm, or other sensitive topics in the storyline. Players who may be sensitive to scenes of fast-paced, competitive violence or depictions of mechanical destruction should take this into consideration before playing.
