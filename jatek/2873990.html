Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

While there is no mature content in the base game, Say is a platform where users share the narrative worlds they are building with other users. While we strive to make the experience suitable for people age 15 and above, it is possible for a player to stumble upon inappropriate user-generated content that has not yet been flagged. We appreciate your help in keeping the platform in accordance with our community guidelines by reporting any violations you might encounter.
