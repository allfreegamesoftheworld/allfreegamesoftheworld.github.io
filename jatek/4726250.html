Minimális gépigény

Minimum:
  • Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system
  • OS: Windows 10 64-bit
  • Processor: Modern 6-core / 12-thread x64 CPU
  • Memory: 16 MB RAM
  • Graphics: High-end DirectX 12 GPU (tested minimum: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2080 GPU)
  • DirectX: Version 12
  • Storage: 130 GB available space
  • Sound Card: DirectX-compatible
  • VR Support: None
  • Additional Notes: SSD strongly recommended. Runtime playback streams roughly 300 MB/s of simulation data. Lower-end GPUs and lower memory configurations have not yet been broadly validated.
