Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

As far as violence, there are martial arts moves in the game that occur from a fight mode or stance. As far as nudity, there is a strip club where strip dances happen. The strippers are nude. There is a sling bikini you can take off. The alcohol is only shown in a club setting where you buy a VIP table and then a golden bottle is brought to you and poured into your mouth. That is all the mature content.
