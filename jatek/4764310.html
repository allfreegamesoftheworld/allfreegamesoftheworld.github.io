Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

This game contains mild suggestive imagery and dialogue, but no explicit sexual content or nudity of genitalia. Most of the illustrations feature chibi-style characters bathing in various dessert-themed baths. While the scenes may be slightly suggestive in a playful way, there is no sexual interaction or explicit depiction. A few dialogue lines (fewer than 10) contain mild innuendo, but the majority of conversations are wholesome expressions of affection between the main couple. The story focuses on the sweet dates of the protagonist couple. Both main characters have yandere-like personalities and occasionally make violent remarks (e.g., mentioning having eliminated many rivals), but there is no graphic violence, blood, or on-screen harm. In a hidden segment, the player may encounter isolated, non-bloody illustrations of a head, a hand, or a leg. These are metaphorical representations criticizing AI-generated art (commenting on the fragmentation of human creativity) and are not intended as horror content, though some players may find them slightly unsettling.
