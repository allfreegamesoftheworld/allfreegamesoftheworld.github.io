Röviden a játékról

Myth of Empires: Throne is a free-to-play seasonal multiplayer war sandbox. Explore worlds and maps, tame creatures, and ride flying mounts for aerial exploration and combat. Wield 100+ melee weapons and siege engines, build your faction, launch cross-server campaigns, and fight for the throne!
