Röviden a játékról

Silentium 2D is a fun, classy, arcade based game which puts you and a friend in a 1v1 arena. Inspired by various arcade games, Silentium 2D was a hobby project designed to let younger audiences get their hands on a keyboard and quickly learn the intuitive controls.
