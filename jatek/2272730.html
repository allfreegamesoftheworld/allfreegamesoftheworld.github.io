Röviden a játékról

Spaceminers is a free-to-play game where you can win Real Gold. Spaceminers: Gold Runner is a timed racing obstacle course and is the first of three game modes in our Early Access phase that will be released throughout 2023 and 2024. The full Spaceminers game launches in Spring 2025.
