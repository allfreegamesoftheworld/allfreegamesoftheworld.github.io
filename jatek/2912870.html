Röviden a játékról

Ember Point is a red-hot, combat-focused, third-person action game. Play as a disgraced defector, Gravitus, from a militaristic mega-corporation. He is stuck cleaning up the mess his former employers left behind — all while wielding a top secret “cure” for a gruesome disease.

Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

This game features frequent combat scenarios with the main character smashing, kicking, and shooting the enemies. There are visuals of blood splashing. Some finishing moves show enemy dismemberment. Strong language is used in the dialogue.
