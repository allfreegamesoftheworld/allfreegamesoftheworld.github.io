Röviden a játékról

Atelier's Key is a dark-fantasy FPS set in a root-bound Victorian world inhabited by puppets. Severed from a hivemind and armed with a mysterious weapon; shoot, grapple, and unlock your way through this world to save a Father from his own damned creation.
