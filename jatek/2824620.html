Röviden a játékról

A story focused, old school, procedural RPG: raise your banner and bring your house to victory in the unique medieval fantasy setting of the Exile Realms. Start out in questing service, gain companions, followers, power and influence, and ultimately through strength or guile learn to rule the lands.

Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

The Exile Princes includes a considerable amount of combat, generally in the fantasy-heroic vein but with some more detailed written descriptions of character deaths (played largely for emotional rather than visceral reaction). There are also character romances with some written scenes that involve mild physical intimacy.
