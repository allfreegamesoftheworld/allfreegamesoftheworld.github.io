Röviden a játékról

Horror Hotel is a top-down, 2D shooter which pits you against increasingly difficult foes. Defeat your demonic enemies with a combination of spells and your Rune Stone. To be victorious you must take the fight to your enemies by traveling to Hell and destroying twelve demon generals in boss battles.
