Röviden a játékról

Deckweaver: Descent into Chaos is a first person deckbuilding game where the player crafts their abilities using different cards they find in the environment. Obliterate forces of chaos, fighting through corrupt locations in the world before getting the chance to face and defeat Chaos itself.
