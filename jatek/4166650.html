Röviden a játékról

Novel Nova is a science fiction/psychological thriller art piece that treads along the lines of the &quot;.exe&quot; genre while taking inspiration from RPGMaker classics. You're a robot undergoing beta-testing. Is the end goal to ESCAPE THE GAME... or is it to OBEY Dr. (Mari) Machina, your creator?
