Röviden a játékról

QuentBlast is a cool indie RPG, where you bowl to attack! In this thrilling game of chance, the renowned Dr. Quent Blast has taken you on as his apprentice. As he shows you the art and science behind bowling, enjoy snappy, witty dialogue.
