Röviden a játékról

Your Friend, Sam is an adventure/puzzle game where you play as the old friend to a boy who went missing one decade ago. Collect clues, solve puzzles, and defend the forest from corruption to build the story and figure out what happened to Sam in this mystical, discovery based world.
