Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Running Horror contains intense horror elements, including depictions of psychological terror, sustained pursuit by a monstrous creature, and fear-based tension. The game includes disturbing imagery, character death, and themes of mental strain and desperation. While there is no sexual content, drug use, or graphic gore, the atmosphere is designed to provoke a sense of dread, anxiety, and helplessness, which may be distressing to some players.
