Röviden a játékról

Dominator Idle is the ultimate idle game for world domination. Train troops and purchase magical upgrades in order to takeover the entirety of earth. With a powerful prestige system you can grow your power to unfathomable standards allowing you to conquer stronger versions of Earth.
