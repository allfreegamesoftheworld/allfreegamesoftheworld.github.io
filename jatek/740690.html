Röviden a játékról

Plangman is an Apple II-inspired hangman platformer. Take the word-guessing of Wheel of Fortune, mix in Lode Runner-style puzzle platforming, add the storytelling of a text adventure, throw in a dash of physics, and--voila!--you have Plangman.
