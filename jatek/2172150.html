Röviden a játékról

Angel Droid is an unfinished experiment that explores a crafting system inspired by Elder Scrolls, Magicka, Vampire Survivors, and many more. This game is rough around the edges, and now exists as a home to the developer's original soundtrack written 15 years before the game's release.
