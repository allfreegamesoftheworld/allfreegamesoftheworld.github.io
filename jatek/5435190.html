Minimális gépigény

Minimum:
  • Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system
  • OS: Windows 10 64-bit (version 1909 or later)
  • Processor: x64 processor with SSE4.2, 2 cores, 2.0 GHz
  • Memory: 2 GB RAM
  • Graphics: DirectX 11 GPU with compute shader support; integrated graphics are fine
  • DirectX: Version 11
  • Storage: 500 MB available space
  • Sound Card: Any Windows-compatible audio device
  • Additional Notes: The course, the puzzles, games against the engine and two-player games run entirely offline — no account and no connection. Ranked play against other people needs an internet connection and a Steam account. A faster processor or GPU mainly raises the engine's strength ceiling at the top difficulty; the lower difficulties play well on modest hardware.
