Röviden a játékról

UldreVoid is an action game created by a solo South African developer. Set out alone in a chaotic world to discover what lies in the Void, a monumental orb-like energy growth that changes the world around it. You must face the conflict of supernatural change while part of humanity tries to take advantage of it.
