Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

When stars Collide contains science fiction violence including fight sequences and descriptions of characters being attacked. There are visual depictions of mild injuries and some blood but no extreme gore. The game contains harsh language and profanity. There are also depictions of trauma and mental health issues. The game contains some sexual situations and innuendo, as well as non-graphic love scenes, which can be toggled off in the settings screen. There are visual depictions of kissing and characters in undergarments, but no depictions of nudity aside from male shirtlessness.
