Röviden a játékról

What if racism had been almost totally eradicated? From a hopeful future, Roma protagonists Robin, Vanessa and Marie return to painful experiences from their adolescence. An educational, narrative game based on participatory research. Three chapters, up to 15 minutes each.

Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

The game deals with serious issues, such as prejudice, discrimination and racism, but it has not been created to cause distress. It should aid in understanding how people in similar situations might feel.
