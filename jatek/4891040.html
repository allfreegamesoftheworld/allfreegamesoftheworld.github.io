Röviden a játékról

Gods Shall Tremble is a deep, text-based gladiator management sim. Program granular fight plans, balance 16 stats, 39.5 Million Behavioural Combinations and loot 15 Million unique items to compete in multiple PvE modes, PvP leaderboards, and friendly and permadeath tournaments to cement your legacy.
