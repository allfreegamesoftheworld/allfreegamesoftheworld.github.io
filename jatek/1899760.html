Röviden a játékról

Maneater and the Golden Teacher is a dark and emotional 2D point and click visual novel set in the aftermath of nuclear Armageddon. A post-apocalyptic recon unit recruit makes his first voyage to the surface world and questions the reality of his lifestyle and the underground society he grew up in.
