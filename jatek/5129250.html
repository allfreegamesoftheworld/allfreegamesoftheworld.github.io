Röviden a játékról

Bubble Pop is a simple game: Bubbles fall from the top of the screen and you pop them. You can sit back, relax and watch the bubbles fall in relaxed mode or you can amp it up and race against time in Competitive mode. Enjoy the beautiful hand-rendered visuals and music in either mode.
