Röviden a játékról

FERRUM is an award winning, student developed, 10 week Souls-like Boss Fighting prototype made for a university course. Fight your way up along a mountain to a difficult boss, with many enemies in the way. Take over the greatest point of energy harvesting in great, bloody, and atmospheric detail.
