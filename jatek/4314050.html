Röviden a játékról

Soulscroll is a fantasy action roguelite where every run begins with gathering, building, fighting and surviving. Collect scroll currency during your runs, open chests in the main menu, and choose whether to use your rewards in future runs or offer them on the Steam Market.

Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Soulscroll contains fantasy combat and dark fantasy themes. Players fight monsters, creatures, and hostile enemies using weapons and abilities. Enemies can be defeated during combat. The game does not contain sexual content, sexual assault/non-consensual sex, BDSM, gore or dismemberment, drug or alcohol abuse, or self-harm.
