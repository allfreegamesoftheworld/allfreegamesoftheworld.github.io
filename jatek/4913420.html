Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Bear With Me is a dark comedy. Most of its endings are fatal and depict comedic, cartoon-style violence and character death, sometimes with brief stylized blood (e.g. crime-scene and aftermath imagery). The tone is humorous rather than realistic. The game contains no sexual content, nudity, or drug use.
