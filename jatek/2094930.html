Röviden a játékról

Farm Wars is a real-time PVP strategy game where you compete to outwit your rivals. Plant crops during the day, defend your fields, and transform into animals at night to sabotage your rivals' fields. Outsmart your rivals, sabotage their plans, and dominate the battlefield with clever tactics!
