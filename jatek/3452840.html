Röviden a játékról

Primal Fray:Prologue is a turn-based strategy roguelite set in the world of Primal Fray. Discover the origins of the story and witness the beginning of an adventure leading into the main game. Enter the Rift,battle enemies, build a team of Primals, outsmart your foes and uncover secrets of the Rift.
