It has no relations to real world organisation, and does not promote or endorse any particular ideology. What is Josei? Josei (女性), translated as women, is a genre of manga, anime, story or game aimed at mature women from 18 to 40+ late teens to adulthood. Josei stories consist of a more mature storytelling and explore darker themes. Stories also portray a more realistic romance, as opposed to the mostly idealized romance in shoujo manga/games. Since the genre of our games is Josei, the content of our games may be offensive and emotionally unsafe for some readers.
