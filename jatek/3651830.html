Röviden a játékról

Project DINOZ is a found-footage dinosaur horror experience set on an island where UCBC secretly revived and experimented on prehistoric life. Survive as the last witness, relive the terrifying Incident Logs, or lead Delta Team into a blacked-out nightmare to uncover what happened.
