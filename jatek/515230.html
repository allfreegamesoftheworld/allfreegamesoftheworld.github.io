Röviden a játékról

Cleo's Lost Idols is a comedy sandbox physics game where you command workers to create mayhem! Emit fire and lightning, squirt water jets, push boulders and dodge fire traps whilst trying to find the missing statue pieces. There's an in-built video creator and tools to upload straight to YouTube.
