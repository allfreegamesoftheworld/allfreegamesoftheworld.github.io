Röviden a játékról

Grandpa wants two ice creams. Grandma is dead and left you 100 kroner. An absurdist Norwegian first-person shooter in PSX style, about getting two ice creams through the bank, the police, Lånekassa and the cashier lady at KJIWI. Nobody comments on any of it.
