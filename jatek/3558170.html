Röviden a játékról

Maskerade: The Deadpan Cry is a first-person narrative oriented Survival Horror game, where you incarnate a young girl who has been kidnapped and forced to work as a ballerina in a terrific and mysterious cabaret, from which she must find a way to escape from the clutches of the Madame who runs it.
