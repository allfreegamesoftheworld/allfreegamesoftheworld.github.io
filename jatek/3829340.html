Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Although Hotel Hideaway: Avatar Chat contains no graphic violence, gore, or explicit sexual content, it is rated 18+ and designed for an adult audience. All “conflict” is playful—think pillow fights or harmless pranks—with no combat system, blood, or injury depicted. Avatars may wear swimwear or lounge attire, but there is no nudity or sexual acts shown. Because it’s a live-service social MMO, user chat can sometimes feature strong language, suggestive banter, or mature role-play. Virtual venues often include decorative bar counters and pixel “drinks,” but there is no real substance-use mechanics or intoxication effects. We do not depict sexual assault, non-consensual acts, self-harm, or drug abuse in any storyline or event. To maintain a safe environment for our adult community, we employ AI-driven moderation alongside block/ignore/report tools—so you remain in control of your social space. Only for players aged 17 and up who are comfortable with open chat and user-generated content.
