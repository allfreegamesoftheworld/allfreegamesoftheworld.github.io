Röviden a játékról

Ready, set, FLAP! Flex your paddle flapping skills in this casual but engaging arcade game. Imagine pinball, but remove all distractions and just focus on the ball action. Use the paddle to strike the falling balls and make them hit the right targets to earn points, power-ups and achievements!
