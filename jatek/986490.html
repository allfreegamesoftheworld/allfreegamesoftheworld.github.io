Röviden a játékról

Another bad day in the future is an open world VR espionage game. Players undertake jobs for one of four political parties in a flying city. Players traverse the city using arm swing based locomotion, grablines, and can climb all the buildings.

Currently, the basic player model in the game is a guy in his underwear. Additionally, you can shoot people, and eventually there will be gore based evidence.
