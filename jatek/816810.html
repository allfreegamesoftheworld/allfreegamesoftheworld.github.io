Röviden a játékról

&quot;Unlight:SchizoChronicle&quot; is a real time deck building game. Every turn each player purchases cards and fights against monsters. If you purchase a card, if that card gets added to your deck and you win the battle with the monster you get a &quot;sherd point&quot;.
