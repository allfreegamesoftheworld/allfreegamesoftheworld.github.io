Röviden a játékról

Veyrasol is a real-time MMO strategy game set in the Veyra Spiral galaxy. Build colonies, command fleets, forge alliances, and battle for dominance across ~120-day seasons in this free-to-play browser-born strategy experience.

Minimális gépigény

Minimum:
  • OS: Windows 10 64-bit or later
  • Processor: Dual Core 2.0 GHz or better
  • Memory: 4 GB RAM
  • Graphics: WebGL2-capable graphics (Intel HD 4000 or newer integrated GPU is sufficient)
  • Storage: 1 GB available space
  • Sound Card: DirectX or OpenAL compatible sound card
  • Additional Notes: Requires a persistent broadband internet connection (online-only). Cross-platform play with the browser version.
