Röviden a játékról

KIRO II: The Wizards Guild is a fantasy based multiplayer dungeon crawler in which up to six players can join online together to navigate through semi-procedurally generated dungeons using a classic text command system to slay monsters, collect loot, and defeat dungeon bosses.
