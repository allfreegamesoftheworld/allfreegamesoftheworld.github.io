Röviden a játékról

Muppy The Bunny : The Danger of Wishes is a 2D platforming game inspired by many early platformers from the 90's. There is a bunch to do in this game, like running from a giant cat, flying a plane, using a parachute, swimming, driving a mine-cart, and so much more.
