Röviden a játékról

Enthrallor is a oldschool adventure puzzle game set in a world scarred by forgotten mage wars. You play as a young mage trapped inside a sentient, decaying armor called an Enthrallor that refuses to let him go. Are you smart enough to weasel your way out of this?
