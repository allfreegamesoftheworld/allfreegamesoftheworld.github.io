Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Constantine Scores contains cartoonish representations of general acts of violence, such as shooting guns or using melee weapons and cartoonish blood impacts. Missions involve general acts of robbery and the game includes settings to keep corpses around or have them vanish. Generic references to drugs, though nondescript. Inclusion of Low Poly NPCs that represent criminal or mature content, such as prostitutes, gangsters, strippers, and drug producers.
