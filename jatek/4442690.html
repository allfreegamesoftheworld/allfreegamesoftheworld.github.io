Röviden a játékról

In buone mani is a narrative game following community nurse Maria through five home visits. The game has been developed by Whitesock and Università del Piemonte Orientale within the EU funded project Age-It – “Ageing well in an ageing society” - Next Generation EU.

Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

The game includes realistic depictions of vulnerable individuals and sensitive social contexts. Storylines feature elderly patients in distress, people with physical disabilities, injuries, substance dependency issues, and characters facing economic or social hardship. No sexual content or graphic violence is shown.
