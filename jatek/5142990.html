Röviden a játékról

Reclaim a world overrun by a rogue AI in this free-to-play 2.5D isometric MMORPG. Transform into a cyborg and hunt robots and combat drones across New Delhi, Lahore, Dhaka, Manila, Washington, D.C., and beyond. Laria, your AI companion, listens and answers aloud. No time limits or subscriptions.

Minimális gépigény

Minimum:
  • Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system
  • OS: Windows 10 64-bit
  • Processor: 64-bit dual-core 2.0 GHz
  • Memory: 4 GB RAM
  • Graphics: DirectX 11 capable integrated graphics (Intel UHD / AMD Radeon Vega or better)
  • DirectX: Version 11
  • Network: Broadband Internet connection
  • Storage: 1 GB available space
  • Additional Notes: Online-only; a constant internet connection is required to play. A microphone is optional and is used only for voice chat with Laria, the in-game AI companion. There is no player-to-player voice chat.
