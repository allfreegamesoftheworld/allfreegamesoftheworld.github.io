Röviden a játékról

A strategy roguelike shooting game. Play as Egg Kid, helping the demon Egg Overlord defeat monsters. Allocate monsters each round, with stronger monsters offering better rewards. Defeat them in time, then use the leftover time to craft equipment and upgrade your stats to reach the goal.
