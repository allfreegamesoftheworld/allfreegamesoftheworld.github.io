Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

O'BLOCK is a satire about sneaker reselling and extreme wealth. You can knowingly sell counterfeit sneakers, pay bribes, short-sell rival companies and trigger a bank run for profit. Parody versions of famous public figures appear throughout. There is occasional mild language. No violence, sexual content, drugs or gambling.
