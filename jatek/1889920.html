Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

The Prison contains mature content, including instances of extreme violence, strong language, and suggestive themes. Players should be aware that the game also involves topics such as non-consensual sex, drug and alcohol abuse, and self-harm. These elements are integral to the game's narrative and setting, and are designed to create a realistic and immersive experience. Player discretion is advised.
