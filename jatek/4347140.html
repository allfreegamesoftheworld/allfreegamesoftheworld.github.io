Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Reaper’s Ledger contains stylized, non-graphic depictions of death and the afterlife presented in a humorous and fictional way. The game features dark and morbid humor, references to souls, purgatory, and the Grim Reaper, and a gothic fantasy atmosphere. There is no graphic violence, gore, sexual content, drug use, or realistic depictions of harm. All themes are presented in a cartoon-like, exaggerated, and lighthearted manner intended for entertainment rather than realism.
