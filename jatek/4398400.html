Röviden a játékról

This is a virtual cat on your computer desktop. It needs you to buy items for it. To buy these items, you need to earn coins by &quot;working&quot;—the game converts your detected keyboard keystrokes and mouse movement distance into corresponding coins. (no input information will be collected. )
