Röviden a játékról

A murder mystery you talk your way through. Six suspects in a snowed-in inn, one killer, and one scapegoat built to take the fall. No dialogue trees: type or say anything, and they answer, remember, and lie to you. Play solo, or six players where the liars are human. Free.

Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Scapegoat is a murder mystery: a killing has occurred and its aftermath, motives, and suspects are discussed throughout. There is no combat or graphic gore in gameplay. Alcohol is served in the inn setting. NPC dialogue is AI-generated at runtime and is not scripted: it may include moderate profanity and suggestive (never explicit) romantic remarks. A built-in content filter that cannot be disabled blocks explicit sexual content, illegal sexual content, and hate speech before any line is shown.
