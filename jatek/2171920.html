When a star falls in a nearby forest, it spurs Po onto a quest to find that light and bring it into their life. Light &amp; Shadow is a shadow puppet platformer where you face your anxieties, push past depression, and seek moments of joy in a grey world. Finding your way isn't always easy, keep going!
