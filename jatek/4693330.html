Röviden a játékról

Corpo Revenge is a 2D Top-Down shooter with handcrafted levels. Play as Martin seeking revenge of the evil energy drink corporation who poisoned his son. Shoot your way through evil guards and avoid hitting innocent office workers on your way to the corrupted CEO.
