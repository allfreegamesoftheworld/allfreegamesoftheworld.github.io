Röviden a játékról

Astonia Resurgence is an isometric 2.5D MMORPG set in a Medieval world with swords and magic. Be a Warrior, specializing in melee combat, or a Mage, specializing in casting spells. If you are able to beat the Gatekeeper himself, you may become a Seyan'Du, gaining all abilities of Warriors and Mages.
