Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

There is strong language, including sexual innuendo (this includes audio). There is audio of sexual moans (male and female), although they are more comedic than realistic. There is a scene that shows a very pixelated nude man (just a single pink pixel is shown for genitals). There is a scene that has some blood at the bottom of a trashcan. There is a scene where the player gets assassinated, but there is no blood or weapons shown and the character simply has X's for eyes when shot.
