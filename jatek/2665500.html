Röviden a játékról

Pool, underwater! A billiards game where your cue ball is a probe and the 6 numbered balls are sea mines. Sink them across 14 differently shaped tables, chase pocket multipliers and pick one of 3 captains who each bend the rules. Free, single-player, for all ages.

Minimális gépigény

Minimum:
  • Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system
  • OS: Windows 10
  • Processor: 64-bit x86 (x86-64) with AVX: Intel Core 2nd generation (Sandy Bridge, 2011) or newer, AMD FX / Bulldozer (2011) or newer; every Ryzen. (Intel Pentium, Celeron and Atom models before 2022 mostly have no AVX.)
  • Memory: 4 GB RAM
  • Graphics: OpenGL 2.1 or newer from the graphics card's driver (NVIDIA, AMD or Intel. Install the vendor's driver for Windows)
  • Storage: 251 MB available space
  • Sound Card: Any sound device (WASAPI)
