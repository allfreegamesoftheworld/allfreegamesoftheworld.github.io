Röviden a játékról

The Museum of Hauntology stands as a monument to a future that never was. Featuring counterfactual digital artefacts drawn from an alternative 1970s that did not quite happen. The work is intended to prompt contemplation of the recent past and the type of future in which we now find ourselves.
