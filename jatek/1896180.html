Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

This game contains violence in a comedic and exaggerated manner. Players will engage in quick-time events that result in over-the-top ragdoll physics, where characters are humorously launched across the room after being punched, kicked, slapped, or shot (without visible blood or gore). The game does not depict realistic violence, serious injury, or explicit content, but due to its goofy physical confrontations, it may not be suitable for all audiences.
