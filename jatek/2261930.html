Röviden a játékról

Altered Algorithm is an action-thriller VR experience blending the craft of film and games in a new way. In this story-driven game set in a futuristic 1983 LA, your playground will be detective work, head-to-head combat, and the ability to DYNAMICALLY interact with full motion video characters.
