Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

This game contains moderate violence typical of a first-person shooter. Players engage in team-based combat using firearms and explosives. When characters are hit, a small amount of red blood visual effects is displayed. There is no dismemberment, gore, or graphic depictions of violence. The game does not include any nudity, sexual content, or sensitive themes.
