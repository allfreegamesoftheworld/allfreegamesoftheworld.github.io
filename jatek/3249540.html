Röviden a játékról

Fling with a Tiefling is a short fantasy romance visual novel that follows you, an adventurer whose journey is about to come to an end. With tonight potentially being the last night with your treasured Tiefling companion Karrus, how will you spend it with him?

Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Fling with a Tiefling is 17+ and contains light, but skippable sexual content (similar to Dragon Age and Mass Effect), and brief mention of fantasy racism towards Karrus, who is a Tiefling, and how that experience affected him.
