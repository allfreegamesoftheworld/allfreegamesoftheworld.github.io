Röviden a játékról

Slice, Suffer, Serve! is a 2D slice-and-click game where you play as a chef, preparing dishes for ghost customers. Sell as many dishes as possible and try not to let the customers get angry! What are you waiting for, new employee? Serve spooky dishes to ghostly guests—quickly!!!

Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Slice, Suffer, Serve! features cartoonish depictions of violence, where players will slice human body parts to prepare meals for ghostly customers. The game’s visual style is exaggerated and not realistic, but the themes of death, dismemberment, and gore are present throughout gameplay. There are no depictions of sexual acts, drug/alcohol abuse, self-harm, or any other sensitive topics like sexual assault or non-consensual acts. However, the violence and themes of death may be unsettling for some players.
