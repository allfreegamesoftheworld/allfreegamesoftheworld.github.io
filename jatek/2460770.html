Röviden a játékról

The Complex IV is a turn-based RPG set in the world of Dungeons &amp; Dragons. Using a subset of the SRD 5.1 ruleset, play solo or compete with up to 5 other players in scenarios designed to test your sword and sorcery skills against a relentless horde of monsters.
