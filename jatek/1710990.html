Röviden a játékról

You play as Joy, a lost controller trying to find their buttons back after you've been smashed to pieces. You will have to cross a cyberpunk style city, and a fantasy filled forest using the simple buttons you can find, and some of the more advanced ones. Good luck, and watch out for dragons.
