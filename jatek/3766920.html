Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Cry of Monster contains mature content focused on moderate violence and gore elements, presented in a retro pixel art style that heightens the horror atmosphere. Players will encounter combat scenes against deformed creatures, with depictions of blood and graphically intense deaths that occur when failing puzzles or being caught by enemies, reinforcing the tension of the survival horror experience. The narrative explores a dark story about failed scientific experiments, with an oppressive atmosphere and psychological horror elements that may be unsettling for some players. There are no depictions of sexual content, sexual abuse, non-consensual sex, drug or alcohol abuse, or self-harm in the game. However, the combination of violence, gore, and themes of human experimentation may not be suitable for all audiences, particularly those sensitive to blood or psychologically disturbing environments.
