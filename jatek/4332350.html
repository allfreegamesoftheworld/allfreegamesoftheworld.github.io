Röviden a játékról

A fast paced, roguelike hack'n slash set in a future where Art Nouveau has had a resurgence. Forgotten vengeful gods return to the mortal plane to claim the worship they think they deserve. And only you, a librarian coming from a long line of godhunters can stop them.
