Röviden a játékról

Hit… or die. Welcome to the twisted version of a classic arcade experience. In We Want Your Head, you enter a world of pixelated nightmares where disembodied heads burst out from the darkness. With every swing of your hammer, you fight for a few more seconds of peace — and for your own head.

Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

This game contains stylized gore, body horror and blood spills. Low poly models and low resolution textures imitate realistic assets. Game shows stylized violence like blood splats, crushing heads etc. During game when player loses, they will encounter a loud jumpscare.
