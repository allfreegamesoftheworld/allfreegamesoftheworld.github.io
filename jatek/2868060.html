Röviden a játékról

King of Hell is a 6-player, online, Arcade Party Game. You play against your friends in a arena filled with random Items and Jump'n Run obstacles. Try to knock your opponents into the lava to stash points and then climb up to the top platform, to score your points and become King of Hell!
