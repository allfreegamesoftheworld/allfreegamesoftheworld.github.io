Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

"Body of Mine" explores themes of gender dysphoria which can be triggering for some audiences. The experience combines digital humans, depicted in underwear, with audio interviews from the trans communities. Some of these interviews include conversations about intimate body parts, sexual themes, and profanity.
