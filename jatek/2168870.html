Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Throughout the game, the player follows the main protagonist on his journey into the abyss. He will engage with a female barmaid and experience sexual encounters. There are fistfights and alcohol consumption in the game. As the game progresses, suicide and blood can be seen.
