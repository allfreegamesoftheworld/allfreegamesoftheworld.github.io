Röviden a játékról

Devil's Wind Mine is a game where you play as an engineer controlling the pumps, seeking to save the son of a desperate mother. You are the decider of this story. It is your duty, as an engineer, to save as many miners as possible, or just one; the devil waits to be fed inside these passages.
