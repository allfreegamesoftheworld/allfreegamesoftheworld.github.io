Röviden a játékról

One Death at a Time is a first-person exploration game. You play as John, in the middle of a midlife crisis. A suspicious milk carton offers you a wish in exchange for 14 souls… but because you're a good person, you'll have to die yourself and explore the city in search of 14 ways to do it.
