Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

This game contains brief, comedic instances of slapstick violence and non-sexual nudity. All characters appearing in this game are 18 years old or older. A character appearing in some bonus scenes is depicted with faded self-harm scars, which are not mentioned in dialogue or narration. Trigger warnings are provided in-game before the character's appearance, and the scenes involving them can be skipped.
