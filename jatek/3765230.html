Röviden a játékról

Zombie Ants VR is a short educational experience that takes the player through the life cycle of Ophiocordyceps, a fungus that manipulates the behavior of insects in order to increase the spread of its spores. This &quot;zombie fungus&quot; was a large inspiration for the video game series Last of Us!
