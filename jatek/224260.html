Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Game involves killing and being attacked by zombies. Some elements of gore (headshots on zombies cause head gore). Attacks & weapon impacts show blood effects, and the player's hands/weapon can get covered in blood. Ability to commit suicide to prevent turning into a zombie due to infection.
