Röviden a játékról

The last Blossom it's a short game scene, based on tabletop roleplaying games. It presents some story, and it's up to you to choose how it will go. Dice rolls and their consequences, tough negotiations, turn-based combat, adventurous atmosphere, and, of course, some dancing lights!
