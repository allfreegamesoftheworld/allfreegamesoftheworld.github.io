Röviden a játékról

Hauntrick is a chaotic, goofy horror game where you hunt cursed objects, perform rituals, and survive bizarre hauntings—solo or with friends. The ghosts? Unpredictable, absurd, and hilariously annoying. Every match is a mix of strategy, panic, and laughter as reality itself starts to unravel.
