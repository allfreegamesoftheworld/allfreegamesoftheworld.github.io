Röviden a játékról

Grim is a horror game inspired from Taiwanese folklore “Aunt Tiger”. The player will play in a room-oriented 3rd-person perspective as a “ghost” and will observe the world from fixed camera perspectives to solve puzzles and survive the monster’s pursuit.
