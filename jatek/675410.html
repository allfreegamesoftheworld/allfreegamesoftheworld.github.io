Röviden a játékról

Eternal Essence is a turn-based collectible card game which pits two opponents against each other. Players construct decks of 40 cards and utilize their limited Rift Power (mana) to summon creatures, cast spells, and equip relics, with the goal of destroying the opponent's Rifter (hero).
