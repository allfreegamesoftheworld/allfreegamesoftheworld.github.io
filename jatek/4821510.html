Röviden a játékról

A hand-drawn 2D sandbox game about being a dog in a dog park. Run around, play fetch, chase squirrels, sniff around, and most importantly, bark at everything. Interact with a weird and wacky cast of dogs, gossip with the local stray cat, and keep an eye out for Krebert the Chihuahua. He's vicious.
