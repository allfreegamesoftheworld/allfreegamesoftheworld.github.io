Röviden a játékról

Solve scrambled anime artwork and watch it come to life. Bikini Heaven is a free swap puzzle game where you rearrange any two tiles to reconstruct beautiful pin-up illustrations — then unlock animated reward videos. Relax or race the clock across grids from 3×3 to 16×16.
