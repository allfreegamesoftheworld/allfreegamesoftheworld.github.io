Röviden a játékról

Invasive Species is a couch co-op tower defense game where you and your friend plant towers in pots to protect your grandparents greenhouse from the robot invasion! Get scrap from defeating enemies and buy upgrades in the catalogue to fend off against more difficult waves of enemies.
