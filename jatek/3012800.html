Röviden a játékról

Leglakh Prologue is a single player tribute to Tom Proudfoots 1994 classic Nahlakh. Form your party of eight champions from knights, archers, mages, and clerics. Fight against hordes of various enemies to gather what is needed to defeat the prime demon Leglakh.
