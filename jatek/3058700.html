Röviden a játékról

Banana Cat is a simple clicker in which you click a “catnana” (a fusion from a cat and a banana). If you wait a specific amount of hours you’ll obtain cat bananas, some cat bananas will also be drawn by YOU in our discord server. What are you waiting for? Come and click banana cats with us!
