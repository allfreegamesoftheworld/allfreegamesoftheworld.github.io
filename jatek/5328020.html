Röviden a játékról

Memórias de Arassay is a pixel art game based on legends from Guarapuava, Brazil. Journey through 2D platforming stages inspired by the Lagoa das Lágrimas, the Capela do Degolado and the serpent beneath the Cathedral, in a retro style that brings players closer to local memory.
