Röviden a játékról

J.M.Rhythm is a black-and-white style rhythm game set in the southern regions of Yangtze River of China. It is the first work produced by J.M.Arcady. In this game, you can use our “rhythm generator” to upload your own music and it will generate a corresponding beatmap automatically.
