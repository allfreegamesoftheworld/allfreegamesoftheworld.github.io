Röviden a játékról

Bubba Yuga is a 2-player couch co-op puzzle game based on the Slavic folktales of the Baba Yaga. Playing as a pair of tied together witches, you and a friend must work together with your walking house to capture all the woodland children and break the curse that’s been placed upon your family.
