Röviden a játékról

Dark Traveller is an action-packed puzzle game with multiple storylines and open endings. The game tells the story of a world on the brink of apocalypse in the distant future. In the world, you travel through quantum space-time to rescue your homeland on the rocks with the help of your partner!
