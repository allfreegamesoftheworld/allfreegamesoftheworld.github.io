Röviden a játékról

Mannequin Syndrome is a third-person action game with horror elements, playable solo or in 1–4 player online co-op. Enter the Syntrax Facility, complete dangerous objectives, restore critical systems, initiate the shutdown, and escape as the mannequin threat grows increasingly aggressive.
