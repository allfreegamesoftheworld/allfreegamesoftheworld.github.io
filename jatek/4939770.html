Röviden a játékról

Humanity's collective doomscrolling feeds the hollow Hueless, draining all color. The world's survival now rests on Clio, a young chromatist student. By channeling her emotions into her Prismal companion, Lochmoo, she grants him the power needed to defeat the Hueless and restore color to the world.
