Röviden a játékról

In Tukki &amp; Champ a chameleon rides a toucan and they compete for food in an asymmetrical local 2-player matchup. Fly strategically as Tukki or aim precisely as Champ to grab more food than your opponent, in a game where you and your adversary move as one.
