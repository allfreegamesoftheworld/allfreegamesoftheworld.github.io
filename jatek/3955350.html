Röviden a játékról

50 Minutes 'Til Impact is set in a retro-futuristic world aboard an advanced, yet luxurious space train! It's a linear narrative adventure where players utilize 3D exploration, code solving, and visual novel interactions to do whatever they can to save everyone on board before time runs out.

Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

50 Minutes Til Impact has many high stakes of life-and-death. Although there may be some violent acts, we do not go into detail nor attempt to make your narrative experience unpleasant to the eyes or mind.
