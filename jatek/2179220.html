Röviden a játékról

Around Us is a 2d prop hunt party game. Prop hunt is similar to hide and seek where you can play among your friends up to 8 players, players are randomly selected to be a hunter or a hider. Hiders can switch into Props and finish tasks, and hunters have to hunt them down.
