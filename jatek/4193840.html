Röviden a játékról

EvoSprout is a casual, stress-free, small-scale farming simulator in a cozy low-poly environment. Manage your character's private garden-farm from a top-down perspective: Buy crops and upgrades, plant and harvest from your plots, and use your different stations to process and analyse your yield.
