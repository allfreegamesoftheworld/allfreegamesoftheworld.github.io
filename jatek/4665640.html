Röviden a játékról

Free to play. A flashy, bizarre competitive TPS built around suitcases. Your suitcase is a shield that blocks bullets, a weapon to slam into enemies, a bomb to throw, and a ride to fly across the battlefield. Some agents' suitcases even spew fire and poison gas. Shoot. Defend. Throw. Fly.
