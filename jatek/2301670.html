Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

The first time you run AI Solitaire you will be directed to the Parental Controls menu. There you can set the allowable content level that will be seen in the game. You may protect these settings with a password, or you can permanently change them so that they can only be changed by uninstalling and reinstalling the game. AI Solitaire is intended to be a celebration of AI Generated Art. The content often includes beautiful women or handsome men. The outfits and costumes can be revealing. Various art styles may feature exagerated proportions. Swimsuits and sexy clothing will always have a minimum of a Teen+ rating. Overly exagerated female proportions or wearing lingerie will be rated Adult. The base game doesn't contain any nudity or Adult rated decks. The "Steampunk Beauties" deck is about as racy as a Teen+ rated deck would get. The base game contains mainly content for people who enjoy looking at women, although there are several Family Friendly card decks included, as well.
