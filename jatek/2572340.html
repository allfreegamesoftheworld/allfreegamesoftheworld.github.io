Röviden a játékról

Franzen is a turn-based RPG set in a dystopian-steampunk world. Explore themes of empathy and politics as Franz and Lefsa Dellia work to uncover the truth of &quot;The Scream&quot;. Meet a cast of colorful characters and fight bizarre creatures in your journey through the world of Branzen!

Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Franzen explores themes of social degradation, genocide, racism, and depictions of violence against fantasy characters. Some creatures and characters have nude bodies, but are not designed for titillation.
