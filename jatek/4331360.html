Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

UMBRA contains horror and survival elements that may be disturbing to younger or sensitive players. This includes: Violence: Players encounter monstrous creatures that must be fought or avoided using light mechanics. There are depictions of attacks and injuries, but no realistic gore or explicit blood. Fear and tension: The game uses dark atmospheres, jump scares, and suspenseful situations to create a sense of danger. Threatening scenarios: Players must sneak through dark corridors, avoid deadly hazards, and make critical survival decisions. Not included: UMBRA contains no sexual content, non-consensual scenes, drug or alcohol abuse, or self-harm. The game is intended for players who can handle intense horror, survival tension, and a dark sci-fi atmosphere.
