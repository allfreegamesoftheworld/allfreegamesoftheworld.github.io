Röviden a játékról

An online multiplayer pixel-art game on one shared 1920 x 1080 canvas. Place a pixel, wait for the cooldown, then contribute again. Build on others' designs or overwrite them—and expect yours to change too. Discuss the canvas through comments and replay its history. Internet required.
