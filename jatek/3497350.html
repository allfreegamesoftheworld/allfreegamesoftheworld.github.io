Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Gladiator Rumble features cartoon-style depictions of violence within its AI-controlled battles. Gladiators engage in combat with weapons such as swords, maces, and axes, resulting in visual effects like exaggerated knockbacks, sparks, and minimal blood splatter. The game is intended to be lighthearted and entertaining, with no graphic depictions of gore, dismemberment, or realistic injuries. The tone is more akin to a competitive, fun arena fight rather than a brutal or realistic simulation of violence. There are no depictions of sexual acts, drug or alcohol use, or any themes involving self-harm, assault, or other sensitive topics in the game.
