Röviden a játékról

Project Search is a visual novel of Chinese immortality cultivation. Scholar Qian Jiangmin investigates collapsed pagodas in Linhai's old town while searching for his missing brother. With the help of Daoist cultivators, he explores grotto-heavens and uncovers the truth behind his double vision.
