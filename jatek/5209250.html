Röviden a játékról

KING 4 A DAY is a small, straightforward roguelike strategy game inspired by chess. Lead four humble pawns across procedurally generated levels, adapt to ever-changing laws, and brave a kingdom of black-and-white roads to rescue the kidnapped White King.
