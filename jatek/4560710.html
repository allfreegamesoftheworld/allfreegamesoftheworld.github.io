Röviden a játékról

What am I? Where am I? Who am I? Spacebolt is a narrative-driven puzzle adventure game where you control a spark of electricity. Traverse walls and ceilings, travel through circuits, and awaken dormant machines as each discovery brings you closer to the truth.
