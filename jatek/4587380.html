Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

This game contains intense psychological horror elements, including dark environments, disturbing scenes, and sudden jump scares. Players may encounter depictions of violence, dead bodies, and unsettling audio-visual effects designed to create fear and tension. The game focuses on atmosphere and storytelling rather than graphic gore. There is no sexual content present.
