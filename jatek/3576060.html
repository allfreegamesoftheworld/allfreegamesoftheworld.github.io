Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Project Origin contains fantasy violence and combat. The game features melee and ranged battles against enemy creatures and humanoid opponents. The Prologue sequence includes cinematic depictions of battle, where players and NPCs engage in combat with swords, magic, and other weapons. There is no sexual content, drug use, or extreme graphic violence beyond standard fantasy RPG combat.
