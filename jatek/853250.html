Röviden a játékról

'신기록 : A Compendium of Ghosts' is a role-playing game based on the webtoon of the same name. Sang returns home as a fully fledged hunter. On his way, he gets caught up in a very strange event. You have to accompany him on his journey to gather clues and puzzle out what is going on.
