Röviden a játékról

Streamers are pitted against their viewers. Streamers sail to various locations in search of gold to plunder. As the viewers watch, the streamer raids the locations and the viewers choose to aid in the defense. With cannons, spells, and teamwork, the viewers can fend off the streamer's attack.
