Röviden a játékról

Beset is a top-down arena brawler. Build a 4-slot loadout from 75 abilities, read your opponents, and wipe them out — across five modes, twelve battlefields, and an opt-in ranked ladder. Online with up to 20 players. No bots. Free to play. No pay to win.
