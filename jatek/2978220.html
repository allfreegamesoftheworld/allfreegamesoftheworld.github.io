Röviden a játékról

Onekind is a 3-10 player social deduction game set on a farm. Among the peaceful animals, there are evil creatures hiding, causing chaos and messing with the hunter’s mind. Luckily, there’s a hunter, a former wizard, ready to hunt them down. Get ready for some fun!
