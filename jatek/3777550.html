Röviden a játékról

Ants: Leaf-Al Warfare is a 2D turn based strategy game where you must command your army of Ants, headed by a Queen, against up to three other foes, being wary of the various environmental hazards. Putting your tactical prowess to good use is the key to victory. War has never been so Leaf-al.
