Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Highrise features an open social community where players can engage in freeform conversation. Some discussions may touch on sensitive or mature themes. Cosmetic items in the game may include avatars wearing bikinis or shirtless male appearances, but full cartoon nudity is never allowed. Players may use mild profanity in chat as long as it is not directed at others in an abusive or harassing manner. Violence in Highrise is lighthearted and cartoonish, often shown through exaggerated animations, whimsical effects, or stylized comic visuals rather than realistic depictions. References to smoking or drinking may appear indirectly through collectible items or community discussions, but illegal activity is not permitted or endorsed.
