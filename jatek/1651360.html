FISTICUBES! THE ULTIMATE ONE-BUTTON BOXING BASHER! Fisticubes is a goofy physics-based one-button boxing party game where up to 4 players compete to destroy each other! Avoid anvils &amp; bombs, eat vegetables and use springs to turn the tide of the fight!
