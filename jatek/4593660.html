Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Totally Normal Meat Co. features a dark humor premise involving the management of a fast-food empire where fictional, cute monster pets are "processed" into food products. While the art style is stylized and cozy, the game depicts cartoonish violence through the automated grinding or harvesting of these creatures. There are no depictions of sexual acts, sexual assault, drug or alcohol abuse, or self-harm. The mature themes are strictly limited to the dark comedic portrayal of "factory farming" in a fantasy setting.
