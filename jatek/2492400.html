Röviden a játékról

Imagine, you are a mole armed with a hammer, battling creatures coming out of holes by throwing your hammer to knock them off the stage. Dig into the environment to avoid hammers and use speed to dash into opponents for a powerful knockback. Be the last one standing and BONK!
