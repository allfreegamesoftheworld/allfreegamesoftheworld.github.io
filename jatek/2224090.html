Röviden a játékról

Kairos is an RTS game set in a medieval fantasy world consumed by Corruption. As a last bastion of life, you must build bases and lead your armies to fight the endless hunger. Discover new magics to purify the land and reclaim what was lost. At the end of times, will you win? Or you will die trying?
