Röviden a játékról

Mnamosyna is an action roguelike game where you challenge dungeons to find your memories stolen by a dragon. Choose from four weapons with different attributes and learn skills that you can use to defeat enemies and use the memories you recover to create combo attacks that combine attack and evasion
