Minimális gépigény

Minimum:
  • Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system
  • OS: Windows 10 (64-bit)
  • Processor: Intel Core i3-6100 / AMD FX-6300 or equivalent (x86_64, SSE4.2)
  • Memory: 4 GB RAM
  • Graphics: Vulkan 1.0 or Direct3D 12 (feature level 12_0) capable GPU, 1 GB VRAM Intel HD Graphics 510 / AMD Radeon R5 or equivalent
  • DirectX: Version 12
  • Network: Broadband Internet connection
  • Storage: 1 GB available space
  • Sound Card: DirectX-compatible sound card
  • Additional Notes: Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system. Keyboard and mouse controls only. Internet connection required for online multiplayer. Single-player with bots can be played offline.
