Röviden a játékról

Hire a hitman, learn to go backwards, buy property to hide, and learn to attack. Acquire currency around the board to buy power ups and bribe the enemy, as well as hide and smuggle. Careful, if you bribe the enemy too often, another enemy will show up on the board. Win by killing all the enemies.
