Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

No More Furries is a Stylized First Person Survival Shooter Depicting guns, explosions and crude humor which is conveyed in a comic setting. The only enemy type in this game are fantasy werewolves which are subject to being shot, blown up, or defeated in violent but cartoonish fashion.
