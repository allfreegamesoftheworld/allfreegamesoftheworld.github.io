Röviden a játékról

SCP: ReEnter is a free, asymmetrical multiplayer FPS set during a massive containment breach. Players are randomly assigned to Humans or SCPs. Fight to secure the facility, escape, or slaughter everyone with seven playable anomalies.

Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

This game contains frequent and intense violence and blood. Players engage in combat using firearms, and monstrous entities (SCPs) attack and kill human characters in graphic ways. The game also features strong psychological horror elements, including jump scares, dark and oppressive environments, and a pervasive atmosphere of fear and dread. As an online multiplayer game, players may also be exposed to strong language from other users via voice or text chat.
