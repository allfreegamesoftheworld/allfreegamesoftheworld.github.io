  • OS: Windows 10 64-bit
  • Processor: Intel Core i3-6100 / AMD FX-6300
  • Memory: 8 GB RAM
  • Graphics: OpenGL 3.3-compatible GPU with 1 GB VRAM (GeForce GTX 650 / Radeon HD 7770 or equivalent)
