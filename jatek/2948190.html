Röviden a játékról

World of Sea Battle is a free-to-play open-world MMO set in the Age of Sail. Catch the wind and fire a broadside before your enemy does. Rise from a schooner to a ship of the line through PvP and PvE battles, trade, explore the Endless Sea and join guild wars for the Archipelago.
