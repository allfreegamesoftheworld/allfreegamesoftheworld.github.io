Röviden a játékról

Get rid of all your cards as quickly as possible. The one left with 0 cards wins. It's tactical and requires a bit of luck. A game is between 2-5 minutes. Ace is the lowest, then 2, 3 etc. J, Q, K = King is the highest. Clear the deck: with a 10 or 4 cards of the same rank. Make some legendary games
