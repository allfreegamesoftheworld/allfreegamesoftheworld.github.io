Röviden a játékról

Wanech’s C.A.N. becomes a retro green pub serving brew, burgers, fries, and wings to synthwave beats. In this top-down cooking sim, prep meals by hitting W/A/S/D in sequence as orders rain down. Keep plates moving, customers smiling, and the pub buzzing!
