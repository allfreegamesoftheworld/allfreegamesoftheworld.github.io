Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

This game contains themes of psychological distress, mental instability, and implied violence. Certain scenes include mild depictions of blood and disturbing imagery related to workplace accidents. The story explores manipulation, fear, and oppression under an authoritarian regime. There are no depictions of sexual content, drug abuse, or explicit physical violence. The mature themes are presented through atmosphere, narrative, and environmental storytelling rather than graphic scenes.
