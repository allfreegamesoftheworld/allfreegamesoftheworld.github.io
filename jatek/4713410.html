Röviden a játékról

Dive into madness in CTHULOOP, a 1st-person cosmic horror adventure game. Trapped in a loop, you must observe eerie anomalies and piece together cryptic clues before your sanity fractures completely. Can you escape, or will you loop forever? Developed for the GMTK 2025 Game Jam (theme: &quot;Loop&quot;).
