Röviden a játékról

Duskborn is a 3rd-person roguelite game. Descend into the Abyss as fast as possible, level up by defeating various enemies, collect and combine items to upgrade your player. Maneuver through the Abyss with your jetpack and grappling hook. The race against time is on.
