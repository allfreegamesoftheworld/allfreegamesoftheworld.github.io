Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Altered Bloodlines contains mature language, nudity, and graphic depictions of oral, vaginal, and other sexual activity. All sexual situations are depicted as occurring between consenting adults. The antagonists of Altered Bloodlines are a vast criminal organization that is involved in kidnapping and will resort to assassinating their own members to protect their secrets. This leads to many depictions of violence throughout the game, including the suicide of a corrupt FBI agent in future DLC. This game includes adult themes, and is intended for adults 18 years and older. The characters, story, and events are works of fiction.
