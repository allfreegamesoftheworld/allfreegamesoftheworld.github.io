Röviden a játékról

Bloxzer is a fun, puzzle, logic, brain-teaser game. Use your brain to solve levels by rolling the block into the green hole. Activate bridges and even use teleportation when available! Levels 1-15 are free, levels 16-30 are premium, but generally more fun and challenging.
