Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

This game is a criminal court simulation based on real Korean criminal law, covering 43 cases involving offenses such as murder, robbery resulting in death, and sexual assault. Case descriptions include non-visual, text-based references to sexual assault and mentions of illegal drug use. Violent or disturbing events are not depicted graphically; they are conveyed through pixel-art characters and text-based indictments/verdicts rather than explicit imagery.
