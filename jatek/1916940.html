Röviden a játékról

Harriet: From Slave to Hero is based on the life of Harriet Tubman, an African-American slave who would become the most famous fighter for the freedom of American slaves, in the years before the American Civil War. It is a 2.5 D turn-based strategy game with puzzle and stealth components.
