Röviden a játékról

CLETTER Homophone Word Spelling pictures English sounds. 7-color coding (colorful phonics) shows patterns. Spell right → words pop. Each level: tap letters, robot carries them, showing growth + 12 achievements. Switch CN/EN UI. Charts spot weak words &amp; progress. PC numpad adds fun.
