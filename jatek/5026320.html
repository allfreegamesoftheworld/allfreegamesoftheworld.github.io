Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Costa Nostra is an online strategy game about organized crime featuring non-graphic combat, weapons, casualties, extortion, theft, sabotage, smuggling and corruption. It depicts a clandestine casino with cards, gambling chips and alcohol bottles, and includes an original strategy card game with wagers using gameplay-earned currency. There is no blood, gore, nudity, sexual content or illegal drug use. The game includes filtered text chat, online interaction and optional purchases of fixed amounts of premium currency with no randomized paid rewards.
