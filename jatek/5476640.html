Röviden a játékról

FIRE CHESS is a serious strategy game that puts players in command of an emergency-response team to contain spreading wildfires. Recruit and manage units across a hex-grid map, use your limited resources to contain a spreading fire, protect residential communities, and respond to critical alerts.
