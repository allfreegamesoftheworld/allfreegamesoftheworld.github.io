Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

The "mature content description" / age restrictions on this simulation are based on: (1) nudity in models of paintings and sculptures which is similar to what would typically be seen in a museum or art gallery; (2) one of the exhibits at the exhibition was a wax model of a dissected head which invokes a rating due to violence (corpses).
