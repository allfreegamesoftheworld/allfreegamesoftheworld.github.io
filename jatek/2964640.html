Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

This game features a unique and immersive system where various substances, including illegal drugs like steroids, unconventional items such as gasoline, and legal substances like cigarettes and strong alcohol, act as "potions." These items can either boost or negate specific player stats, adding a strategic layer to gameplay. Additionally, players can discover hidden Easter eggs that lead to unexpected and sometimes fatal consequences for certain NPCs.
