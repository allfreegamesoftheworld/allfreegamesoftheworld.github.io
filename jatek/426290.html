Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

All depictions of violence are done through text, including character deaths, which can occur frequently. The Away Team is a game about surviving with limited resources. Starvation and cannibalism are possible outcomes of that. Addiction is a trait of one of the characters in the game, though no illegal drug use is depicted. There are hallucinogenic substances on some alien planets. Some characters in the game can profanity on rare occasions, to emphasize their reactions to dramatic moments within the game, which may not be a part of all playthroughs.
