Röviden a játékról

Night Wish is a 2D top-down roguelite action game combining subculture aesthetics with the core thrills of a twin-stick shooter. Experience the double life of Nia—an ordinary student by day, and an elite agent by night. Breach through relentless bullet hell and shape the story through your choices!
