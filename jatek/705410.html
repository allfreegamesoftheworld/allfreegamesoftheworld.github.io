Röviden a játékról

World War Party: Game of Trump is a deathmatch battle played by two teams of 4 players each (humans or bots). But the teams are not alone on the battlefield: PRESIDENT TRUMP drives around in his mighty ‘murican tank shooting lasers and talking nonsense.
