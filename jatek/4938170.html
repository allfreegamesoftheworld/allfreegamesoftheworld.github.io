Röviden a játékról

Ostfriesland Games turns road bowling, ditch vaulting, mudflat sled racing, shrimp peeling and tea serving into a retro arcade game. With real places in pixel art, local and worldwide high scores, and East Frisian humour. Even making tea can get hectic.
