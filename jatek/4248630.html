Röviden a játékról

Crateball is a 3v3 arcade football game. Crates drop onto the pitch during the match. Some give you a gun, a homing bazooka or a shield. Others freeze you, reverse your controls or explode. The arena changes after every goal. Play online with friends, and bots take any empty spots.
