Röviden a játékról

Tracebound is an atmospheric, fast-paced ice-skating game. Skate, leap, and keep momentum across a bound giant’s body, carving traces to break shackles. Challenges evolve via new traversal patterns and hazards, blending flow-based movement with environmental storytelling.
