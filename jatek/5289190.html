Röviden a játékról

VALHALLA ARCHIVE is a tactical fantasy CCG where matches are decided in 5 turns. Deploy a 20-card deck across front and back rows, master 3 unique win conditions, and conquer the roguelite 'Dimensional Corridor'. Harness unpredictable Mana Rifts and claim your place as the ultimate Archivist!
