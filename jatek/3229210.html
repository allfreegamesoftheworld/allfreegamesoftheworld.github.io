Röviden a játékról

Game Of Thrall'an is action RPG developed by CGArt, Jsc, inspired by the iconic character Thrall from the World of Warcraft universe. Players will take on the role of an Orc character named Thrall'an on a journey to the icy land of Alterrac filled with enemies to destroy the Ice Lord - Lookholar.
