Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Scene No.0 is a psychological horror game focused on a tense and unsettling atmosphere. While there is no extreme gore, minimal depictions of blood splatters may appear as environmental details. The game also includes interactive tobacco-related assets in specific rooms. Players can interact with these items, but the game does not feature any visual depictions or animations of characters actually smoking.
