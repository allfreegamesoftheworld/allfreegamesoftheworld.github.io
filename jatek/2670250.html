Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

Dreamslayer contains highly stylized and exaggerated depictions of blood and gore from enemy demons (blood splatters, dismemberment, death via explosions), as well as horror imagery of demons and demonic settings.

Minimális gépigény

Minimum:
  • Requires a 64-bit processor and operating system
  • OS: Windows 10 64bit
  • Processor: intel i5 8400 or AMD Ryzen 5 2600
  • Memory: 8 GB RAM
  • Graphics: NVIDIA GTX-1060 or AMD Radeon RX 590
  • DirectX: Version 12
  • Storage: 2 GB available space
