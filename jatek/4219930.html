Röviden a játékról

Begin your career at the Multiversal Institute for the Rhythmic Arts (MIRA), an interdimensional school where music, magic, and friendship collide.  Encore! is a rhythm game and visual novel hybrid, where you must curate a band from wacky characters to win Battle of the Bands!
