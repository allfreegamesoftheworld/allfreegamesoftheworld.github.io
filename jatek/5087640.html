Röviden a játékról

Soldier Cats is a round-based 3v3 tactical shooter in the back streets of Istanbul. You only see what your cat's vision cone sees, and the server decides visibility, so nobody peeks through walls. Pick one of six cats, hold the middle, win ten rounds.
