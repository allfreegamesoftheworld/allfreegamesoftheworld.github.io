Tartalmi figyelmeztetés

This game contains stylized combat and historical-fantasy violence. Players use melee weapons, ranged weapons, and abilities to fight regular enemies, elite enemies, and bosses. Combat includes impact effects, damage numbers, and characters being defeated. The game contains no graphic gore, dismemberment, nudity, sexual content, sexual violence, drug abuse, self-harm, or suicide. Its setting includes themes of warfare, frontier conflict, and military defense.
