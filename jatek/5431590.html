Röviden a játékról

TrenchFin is a 3rd person behind the character 3D platformer melee combat game that follows Asha Trenchfin, an anglerfish on a mission to reach the top of a spiraling reef to save the magic coral tree from a terrifying Leviathan by using her light to reveal invisible platforms and enemies.
